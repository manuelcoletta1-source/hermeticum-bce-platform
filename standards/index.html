3. Why technical references are needed

Most digital systems rely on authentication, logs and perimeter security. These are not sufficient for environments requiring deterministic accountability, public/private evidence separation and audit-ready AI workflows.

HBCE references define the minimum R&D structure required for operational identity, proof continuity, AI accountability and public verification without public custody of sensitive identity evidence.

5. Core invariants

  • PRIVATE VERIFICATION SEPARATION — sensitive identity evidence belongs only in the private layer.
  • PUBLIC PROOF MINIMIZATION — public records expose proof references, not identity evidence.
  • HASH-ORIENTED PUBLIC LAYER — public verification uses digest commitments such as payload_sha256.
  • APPEND-ONLY TRAJECTORY — public history should be extended, not silently rewritten.
  • DETERMINISTIC VERIFICATION — verification should return explicit states.
  • NO PUBLIC DATA CUSTODY — no tax codes, document numbers, identity documents or private evidence in public files.
  • FAIL-CLOSED VALIDATION — missing, malformed, ambiguous or forbidden data must not produce validity.
  • HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY — AI outputs remain decision-support unless separately validated by accountable humans.

6. Private verification and public proof

IPR Verified may use private identity verification based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. Accepted private document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license.

This material must not be stored in public GitHub files, public registries, public issues, public demo surfaces or public proof records.

7. EU regulatory alignment posture

HBCE references are designed to be structurally compatible with EU high-accountability principles: minimization, auditability, traceability, human responsibility, operational resilience and controlled AI use.

This is an architecture posture. It is not a formal legal opinion, regulatory certification, conformity assessment, public authority approval, eIDAS qualification, KYC / AML provider status or third-party audit attestation.

8. Adoption logic

Technical references become useful when they reduce ambiguity, lower audit cost and make operational responsibility easier to reconstruct.

  • Lower legal ambiguity through clearer technical evidence.
  • Deterministic audit trails for AI-assisted workflows.
  • Operational identity for human, AI, organizational and process contexts.
  • Public proof without public custody of identity evidence.

HBCE references are open R&D materials and can inform compatible implementations, but this page does not claim official standardization, public authority recognition or regulated status.
