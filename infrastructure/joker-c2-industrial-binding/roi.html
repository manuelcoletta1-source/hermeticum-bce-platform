ROI drivers (why this pays for itself)

1) Downtime reduction

Block bad actions before they break a cell or force a shutdown. Fewer emergency stops triggered by misconfiguration.

2) Liability control

Deterministic traceability of who authorized what. Stronger posture for insurance, disputes, and regulated environments.

3) Faster audits

Evidence packs are structured by design. Verification can be reproduced without relying on trust in internal logs.

4) Contractor governance

Contractors operate under explicit identity and policy. Easy to enforce role-based permissions and approvals.

5) Fleet scaling

The same governance model applies across many robots/sites. Standardize responsibility while your systems scale.

6) Future compliance posture

As autonomy increases, governance requirements grow. You install the accountability layer early instead of patching later.
