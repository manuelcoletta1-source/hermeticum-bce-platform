MATRIX AI Audit Trail

1. Executive summary

MATRIX AI Audit Trail converts AI-assisted document work from an ordinary chat interaction into a traceable, attributable, human-validated and audit-reportable workflow.

The MVP focuses on one concrete use case: AI-assisted analysis of governance, compliance, cybersecurity and audit documents.

It is intentionally narrow. It does not attempt to implement the full MATRIX architecture. It demonstrates one commercially understandable process that can be tested by a real organization.

3. Problem

Companies are starting to use AI for document analysis, compliance support, cybersecurity documentation, governance review, internal audit, policy drafting and operational decision support.

The problem is that ordinary AI use often leaves weak auditability. A team may not be able to prove which document was analyzed, what action was requested, what model or runtime processed it, what risk was assigned, whether a human reviewed the output, and whether a final audit record exists.

This creates a gap between AI usefulness and compliance-grade accountability.

4. Solution

MATRIX AI Audit Trail introduces a simple controlled workflow: 

Document upload
→ AI analysis
→ governance decision
→ human validation
→ EVT event
→ OPC proof
→ audit report

The output is not only an AI response. The output is a structured audit trail that records the document hash, AI action, risk class, runtime decision, human validation, EVT event, OPC proof and final report state.

5. What the MVP already demonstrates

DOCUMENT HASH

Input traceability

The uploaded document is assigned a deterministic hash commitment.

AI ACTION

Controlled analysis

The AI action is recorded as a structured task, with prompt, action type and output hash.

GOVERNANCE

Risk and decision

The workflow assigns risk class, policy status, runtime decision and human oversight requirement.

HUMAN VALIDATION

Responsibility boundary

The AI output does not become final until a human operator approves, corrects or rejects it.

EVT

Event trace

The validated workflow generates an EVT event with a trace hash and verifiable status.

OPC

Operational proof

The EVT is linked to an OPC proof record and exported inside the final audit report.

8. Pilot proposal

After the internal self-pilot, the external pilot format is simple:

  • Duration: 30 days;
  • Users: 1–3 operators;
  • Documents: 10–20 test documents;
  • Workflow: one AI-assisted document analysis process;
  • Output: audit-ready report package;
  • Final deliverable: pilot evaluation and productization roadmap.

This makes the MVP small enough to test while still being concrete enough for commercial validation.

9. Boundaries and non-claims

MATRIX AI Audit Trail is currently an MVP and internal R&D self-pilot.

It does not claim:

  • legal certification;
  • regulatory approval;
  • eIDAS qualification;
  • public-sector adoption;
  • external audit certification;
  • automated compliance;
  • legally binding evidence status by itself;
  • production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness.

It demonstrates traceability, attribution, governance decisioning, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof generation and audit-ready reporting.
