IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case built around IPR — Identity Primary Record, the base operational product of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. Platform. 2. IPR Verified model

IPR Verified gives the project a more professional startup-grade identity model. It follows a private verification logic similar in structure to standard banking and fintech onboarding patterns, while preserving public proof minimization.

The private verification model may use:

  • tax code;
  • identity card / CIE;
  • passport;
  • driving license;
  • private evidence package.

The public layer must not contain tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portrait images, biometric material, private evidence, personal data or personal payloads. 

private verification
→ IPR Verified record
→ hash-oriented proof reference
→ minimized public registry entry
→ verify / audit / EVT / OPC

IPR Verified does not create legal identity, regulated KYC / AML compliance, eIDAS qualification, public authority approval or production authorization by itself.

5. Problem

Companies and institutions increasingly use AI for document analysis, compliance support, cybersecurity documentation, governance review, internal audit, policy drafting and operational decision support.

The problem is that ordinary AI use often leaves weak auditability. A team may not be able to prove which document was analyzed, what action was requested, what model or runtime processed it, what risk was assigned, whether a human reviewed the output, which identity or responsibility context was attached, and whether a final audit record exists.

This creates a gap between AI usefulness and compliance-grade accountability.

6. Solution

IPR AI Audit Trail introduces a simple controlled workflow: 

IPR operational reference
→ document upload
→ document SHA-256
→ AI-assisted analysis
→ governance decision
→ human validation
→ EVT event
→ OPC proof
→ audit-ready report

The output is not only an AI response. The output is a structured audit trail that records the IPR reference, document hash, AI action, risk class, runtime decision, human validation, EVT event, OPC proof and final report state.

10. Pilot proposal

After the internal self-pilot, the external pilot format can remain narrow:

  • Duration: 30 days;
  • Users: 1–3 operators;
  • Documents: 10–20 synthetic, public, owned or expressly authorized test documents;
  • Workflow: one AI-assisted document analysis process;
  • IPR profile: IPR Base, IPR Verified or IPR Professional depending on pilot scope;
  • Output: audit-ready report package;
  • Final deliverable: pilot evaluation and productization roadmap.

This makes the MVP small enough to test while still being concrete enough for commercial validation.
