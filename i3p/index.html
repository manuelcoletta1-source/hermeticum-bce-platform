TORINO R&D ENTRY POINT

Overview

HBCE is an experimental research and development laboratory focused on persistent operational identity infrastructures for autonomous and hybrid systems.

The research explores identity binding, execution authorization and auditability for next-generation machine environments including artificial intelligence, robotics, drones and distributed software agents.

The Missing Layer

Autonomous systems can execute actions but cannot demonstrate persistent operational identity across time, environments and infrastructures.

Current architectures rely on device identifiers, accounts or certificates that do not provide continuous, verifiable identity for autonomous execution.

This structural gap limits secure deployment of:
• robotics and industrial automation
• AI agents and hybrid systems
• distributed machine environments
• human-machine operational continuity

A deterministic identity layer for execution environments is currently absent.

IPR — Identity Primary Record

HBCE develops the Identity Primary Record (IPR): a persistent, append-only operational identity structure designed for autonomous and hybrid entities.

Core properties:
• persistent identity anchoring
• append-only evolution
• fail-closed verification logic
• environment-agnostic structure
• hash-based integrity
• compatible with EU regulatory logic

Execution requires identity. Identity must persist beyond devices and sessions.
