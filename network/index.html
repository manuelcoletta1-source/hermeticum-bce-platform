Territorial node licensing

HBCE nodes are territorial operational gateways for IPR issuance, certified events, and network coordination. Nodes are assigned progressively. Until assignment, operations remain under Origin Node coordination.

Pricing Deployment Operator Zone
Operational rule
Licensing = operational rights on the selected territory.

A licensed node can issue IPR, manage operators, and run certified services within its scope. Until a node is assigned, the territory is operated by the Origin Node (fail-closed governance).
