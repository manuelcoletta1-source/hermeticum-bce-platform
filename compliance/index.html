Compliance posture

HBCE Platform is an experimental R&D public gateway designed to support technical audit reconstruction, accountability, proof-reference verification, operational identity research and responsible AI governance. The current platform is centered on IPR as the operational R&D/MVP product, IPR Verified as the private-verification profile, IPR AI Audit Trail as the first demonstrable use case, HBCE as the operational ecosystem, JOKER-C2 as the runtime demonstrator and MATRIX as the architectural framework.

The platform is designed with an EU-aligned posture: private verification, data minimization, public proof minimization, deterministic verification, append-only evidence logic and fail-closed validation.

This page describes a compliance posture and design direction. It is not a formal certification, legal opinion, regulatory approval, audit attestation, conformity assessment, public authority validation, regulated KYC / AML status, eIDAS qualification or institutional authorization.

Technical evidence does not create automatic compliance

HBCE Platform can support reconstruction, audit review and proof continuity, but it does not automatically make a workflow compliant with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law, KYC / AML obligations, contractual obligations or sector-specific regulation. 1. Compliance status

HBCE Platform is currently an R&D/MVP environment and public documentation gateway. Its compliance posture is designed to support technical auditability, evidence reconstruction, private verification boundaries, public proof minimization and responsible AI governance.

The platform does not provide legal certification, automated compliance, regulatory approval, public-sector adoption, external audit certification, eIDAS qualification, qualified trust-service status, regulated KYC / AML provider status, production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness or legally binding evidentiary status by itself.

Any production deployment, external customer pilot, regulated workflow, public-sector use, institutional integration or real identity-verification operation requires independent legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance, procurement and operational review.

2. System hierarchy

IPR

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is the base operational product. Its compliance role is to support an identity-linked event and proof model, not to replace legal identity systems, public registries, digital signatures or qualified trust services.

IPR Verified

IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence in a controlled private layer. This does not make the platform a regulated KYC / AML provider, identity provider, qualified trust service or public authority registry.

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. It demonstrates how AI-assisted document workflows can produce traceable, human-validated and audit-ready technical records.

HBCE, JOKER-C2 and MATRIX

HBCE is the operational ecosystem for registry, verification, EVT, OPC, policy and fail-closed logic. JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator used to show the workflow in operation. MATRIX is the architectural framework and must not be presented as the immediate regulated product.

3. Core principles

Public surface is minimized

  • Public registry entries use minimized fields such as entity_type, subject_label, payload_sha256, verification_level, timestamp and status.
  • Private release artifacts, tax-code evidence, identity documents, document numbers, customer files, credentials and sensitive payloads must remain outside the public repository.
  • Public matching proves public proof-reference presence only; it does not replace private validation.
PRIVATE_VERIFICATION GDPR_MIN NO_PUBLIC_DATA_CUSTODY

Append-only evidence

  • Public evidence history should be extended, not silently rewritten.
  • Changes should become new entries, events, status updates, findings or audit traces.
  • Append-only discipline supports reconstruction of public proof chronology.
APPEND_ONLY AUDIT_FIRST

Deterministic verification

  • Verification is based on explicit fields and deterministic comparison, not narrative interpretation.
  • Missing or malformed required fields produce INVALID or NON_OPERATIONAL.
  • Same valid input should produce the same verification result.
DETERMINISTIC NO_PARTIAL_VALIDATION

Fail-closed by default

  • If the system cannot verify a required condition, it must not infer validity.
  • Fail-closed behavior is the baseline posture for registry, verification, receipt, audit, private verification and AI-assisted workflows.

5. Compliance interpretation

What public proof can show

  • A public proof reference is present.
  • A hash matches an expected commitment.
  • A timestamped public record exists.
  • A public status field is declared.
  • An EVT or OPC reference may support technical reconstruction.
  • A public audit trace records a remediation or review step.

What public proof does not show by itself

  • It does not certify legal identity.
  • It does not prove regulated KYC / AML compliance.
  • It does not replace private evidence review.
  • It does not create automatic institutional approval.
  • It does not replace qualified trust services or regulated certification.
  • It does not validate private claims without supporting evidence.
  • It does not create automatic compliance with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law or sector-specific regulation.

What must remain private

  • Tax codes and fiscal codes.
  • Identity documents and document numbers.
  • Raw personal identifiers.
  • Credentials, secrets, tokens and private keys.
  • Customer files and confidential third-party data.
  • Private prompts and private outputs.
  • Production logs with personal or sensitive data.
  • Sensitive operational payloads.

6. Operational responsibilities

Issuer / Maintainer

  • Maintains documentation and public proof surfaces.
  • Avoids public custody of private evidence.
  • Avoids public custody of tax-code and identity-document material.
  • Publishes minimized proof records only.
  • Documents changes through commits, audit traces and findings.
  • Maintains clear R&D/MVP boundaries and non-claims.

Verifier

  • Checks deterministic proof fields.
  • Uses payload_sha256 for public proof matching.
  • Denies validity when required evidence or registry state is missing.
  • Does not infer identity, authorization, compliance or legal effect from public proof alone.

Public / Auditor

  • Reads registry as minimized public proof trail.
  • Checks audit findings and remediation history.
  • Understands public proof as technical commitment, not automatic certification.
  • Reviews private evidence only through authorized and lawful channels.

7. Explicit limitations

HBCE Platform does not

  • store public personal data beyond minimized public labels;
  • store tax codes in the public registry;
  • store identity documents in the public registry;
  • replace legal accountability;
  • replace private identity verification;
  • provide legal, tax, cybersecurity or regulatory advice;
  • provide regulated KYC / AML services;
  • perform probabilistic validation where deterministic proof is required;
  • authorize offensive cybersecurity activity;
  • produce automatic compliance by itself.

HBCE Platform assumes

  • private artifacts are protected by the holder or authorized process;
  • public registry entries remain minimized;
  • verification uses canonical deterministic rules;
  • sensitive material is not placed in public repository files, public demos or public issues;
  • legal, contractual, fiscal, cybersecurity, procurement or institutional effects are handled separately where required.
