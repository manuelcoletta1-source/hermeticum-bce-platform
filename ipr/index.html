IPR · IDENTITY PRIMARY RECORD · OPERATIONAL PRODUCT · IPR CARD · EVT · OPC · JOKER-C2

IPR is the operational identity record that enables governed AI access.

IPR — Identity Primary Record is the first operational product of the HBCE Platform. It is designed to connect a subject, a role, an operation, an event, a proof reference, responsibility, continuity and access state into one verifiable operational trace.

IPR is not only a login, not only an account, not only a credential, not only a hash and not only a registry entry. It is the operational identity layer that can generate an IPR Card, connect to an EVT activation event, produce or reference an OPC proof receipt, expose a minimized public proof reference and enable governed access to JOKER-C2 where policy allows it.

The platform rule is structural: Classic AI starts with an email. JOKER-C2 starts with a verified IPR.

What IPR connects

SUBJECT

ROLE

Under which capacity

The record is interpreted through subject type, role, context, organization and responsibility boundary.

What happened

CONTINUITY

How it persists

IPR links events across time so that operations are not isolated fragments but reconstructible sequences.

RESPONSIBILITY

Who owns the act

The record supports reviewable attribution instead of anonymous or unbound execution.

ACCESS

What is allowed

IPR state can determine whether JOKER-C2 access is allowed, denied, suspended, revoked or requires review.

WHY IPR IS DIFFERENT

A login enters a system. IPR governs operational attribution.

A login says who entered a system. A log says that something happened. A signature says who signed. A hash says whether data changed. IPR connects identity, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility, continuity and governed access into one operational record.

Verification levels

L0

Technical R&D reference

Local, synthetic or technical reference used for internal tests, proof-of-concept flows and non-production demonstrations.

L1

Basic account verification

Account and contact-level checks, such as email or phone verification. This does not by itself create document-verified operational identity.

L2

Document-based private verification

Private review of accepted identity-document evidence and controlled linkage to a national tax identifier or national identification number where applicable.

L3

Enhanced operational verification

Enhanced private verification with document evidence, national identifier reference, photo, short video, liveness or manual review in a separately scoped implementation.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION / PUBLIC PROOF

The IPR model separates private verification from public proof. Sensitive identity evidence must remain private or controlled. The public registry should expose only minimized proof references.

LEGAL BOUNDARY

IPR is operational identity, not official identity issuance.

IPR does not replace an identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet or qualified eIDAS certificate. IPR does not create official European identity, legal certification, regulated KYC/AML provider status, public authority validation or qualified trust-service status by itself.

HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems in future compliant integrations. Any production onboarding, document verification, liveness detection, biometric comparison, enterprise deployment, public-sector use or institutional integration requires separate legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and operational review.
