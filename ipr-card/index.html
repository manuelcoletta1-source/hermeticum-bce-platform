IPR CARD · VERIFIED OPERATIONAL ACCESS KEY · EVT · OPC · QR · FAIL-CLOSED

IPR Card is the operational key for governed AI access.

IPR Card is the user-visible operational identity card issued within the HERMETICUM B.C.E. environment. It represents an IPR state, a verification level, an issuer reference, an activation event, an OPC receipt, a public hash reference and a verification surface.

It is not a state identity document, not a passport, not CIE, not SPID, not an EUDI Wallet, not a qualified eIDAS certificate and not a banking card. It is an internal HBCE operational card used to connect verified identity, access control, proof continuity and governed AI workflows.

The access rule is simple: no active verified IPR, no governed JOKER-C2 access.

POSITIONING

The card is operational, not official state identity

IPR Card is designed to make the HBCE access threshold visible. It does not replace official identity systems. It is a controlled operational reference that can be connected to identity evidence, verification state, EVT continuity, OPC proof and public proof minimization.

Status

IPR Card states

PENDING

Verification not complete

IPR_PENDING means that identity evidence has been submitted or prepared, but the subject is not yet fully verified for governed runtime access.

ACTIVE

Access-capable state

IPR_ACTIVE means that the operational identity has been verified inside the HBCE model and can be used for controlled access where policy allows it.

SUSPENDED

Temporary restriction

IPR_SUSPENDED means that access is temporarily restricted due to policy, risk, review, anomaly or operational control.

REVOKED

No valid access

IPR_REVOKED means that the card or operational identity reference is no longer valid for governed access.

EXPIRED

Renewal required

IPR_EXPIRED means that the validity period has ended and a renewal or review process is required before access can be restored.

REVIEW REQUIRED

Human or controlled review

IPR_REVIEW_REQUIRED means that the identity or evidence package requires correction, escalation or manual verification before any full access decision.

ACCESS RULE

JOKER-C2 access is fail-closed

The IPR Card becomes operationally meaningful only when the access state is explicit. Missing, ambiguous, suspended, revoked or expired states must not produce runtime access.

Verification levels

L0

Technical R&D reference

Local, synthetic or technical reference used for internal tests, non-production demonstrations and hash-oriented attribution.

L1

Basic account verification

Basic contact-level checks such as email or phone verification. This level does not by itself create document-verified operational identity.

L2

Document-based private verification

Private review of accepted document evidence and controlled linkage to a national tax identifier or national identification number where applicable.

L3

Enhanced operational verification

Enhanced verification with document evidence, national identifier reference, photo, short video, liveness or manual review in a separately scoped implementation.

LEGAL BOUNDARY

Required public wording

This wording should remain stable across public pages, documentation and pilot materials: 

IPR Card is a digital operational card issued within the HERMETICUM B.C.E. environment.
It does not replace an identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet or qualified eIDAS certificate,
unless future integrations with recognized identity or trust-service systems are separately established.
HBCE does not issue official European identity.
HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems
in future compliant integrations.

Any production onboarding, document verification, liveness detection, biometric comparison, enterprise deployment, public-sector use or institutional integration requires separate legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and operational review.
