PUBLIC-SECTOR PROBLEM

AI-assisted public workflows require traceability.

Public-sector and regulated organizations may increasingly use AI to support document analysis, policy review, cybersecurity documentation, compliance workflows, procurement support, civil-protection documentation and administrative decision support.

Ordinary AI use may not be sufficiently traceable. A team may not be able to reconstruct which document was analyzed, what AI action was requested, what risk class was assigned, whether a human reviewed the output, which IPR reference was attached, which proof references exist and whether a final audit trail exists.

IPR AI Audit Trail addresses this gap by making the AI-assisted workflow reviewable, document-bound, human-validated and reportable.

Public-sector relevant principles

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

AI is decision-support

AI output must not replace human or institutional responsibility. Human validation remains explicit.

AUDITABILITY

Traceable workflow

Document hash, AI action, governance decision, EVT, OPC and report make the workflow reviewable.

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

Missing, malformed or unverifiable records must not produce validity or authorization.

NO-CUSTODY

Public proof only

The public layer must not store private evidence, tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, credentials or sensitive data.

REGISTRY v3

payload_sha256

Public proof records should use minimized registry semantics, verification level, status and payload_sha256.

EU-FIRST

Governance reference

EU-first is a design and governance reference posture, not a formal certification or public authority approval.

PILOT PATH

Start small, non-sensitive and reviewable.

  • duration: 30 days;
  • users: 1–3 authorized operators;
  • office / operator mapping: non-sensitive role definition only;
  • IPR profile: IPR Base, IPR Verified or IPR Professional depending on scope;
  • IPR Card scope: only where verification and access-state review are in scope;
  • documents: 10–20 public, synthetic or explicitly authorized documents;
  • workflow: one AI-assisted document analysis process;
  • JOKER-C2 boundary: runtime discussion only where separately scoped;
  • output: audit-ready report package;
  • review: usability, traceability, risk classification, validation step and report clarity.

Any pilot involving real public-sector data, regulated data, personal data, identity verification or sensitive operational data requires prior legal, security, privacy, procurement and institutional review.

INSTITUTIONAL REVIEW REQUIREMENTS

HBCE does not bypass public-sector procedure.

Any public-sector or public-sector-adjacent pilot must be reviewed through the appropriate procedural route. The platform does not bypass procurement, data-protection, cybersecurity, legal, administrative or institutional controls.

  • legal basis and institutional mandate;
  • data protection and DPIA assessment where required;
  • cybersecurity and access-control review;
  • procurement or innovation-procedure compatibility;
  • human responsibility and validation model;
  • retention, audit and evidence-custody policy;
  • public proof / private evidence separation;
  • JOKER-C2 runtime boundary and access review where applicable.
