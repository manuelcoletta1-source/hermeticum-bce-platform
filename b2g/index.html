Compliance and positioning

HBCE does not replace eIDAS or EUDI wallet identity schemes. It operates as an operational identity and auditability layer for deterministic verification and infrastructure-level traceability.

Public registry is hash-only. No personal data custody. Fail-closed validation ensures deterministic trust.
