Status reports R&D operational posture.

This page summarizes the public R&D status of HBCE Platform. It reports the current platform posture, IPR-first product orientation, IPR Card model, JOKER-C2 runtime demonstrator, Registry / Verify state and legal / privacy / claims boundary.

Status does not certify production readiness, legal validity, cybersecurity assurance, public authority approval, regulated compliance, eIDAS qualification, KYC / AML status, public-sector adoption or institutional deployment.

HBCE Platform is currently a public R&D gateway for operational identity, public proof minimization, audit-ready workflow research and governed AI runtime demonstration.
