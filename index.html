European Operational Identity Infrastructure

HERMETICUM B.C.E. provides the operational identity layer required for AI, companies, institutions and infrastructures across Europe. Each entity operates through a verifiable Identity Primary Record (IPR) with deterministic validation and fail-closed logic.

Infrastructure overview

Operational identity

Every entity — human, company, AI or infrastructure — operates through a certified operational identity. This enables accountability, traceability and deterministic verification.

Deterministic verification

Each IPR is linked to a cryptographic fingerprint and verified against a public append-only registry. If verification fails, execution is blocked (fail-closed).

European infrastructure

The system is designed as a European-scale operational identity infrastructure integrated with enterprises, institutions and government systems.

Hash-only public proof

The public registry stores only hash proofs and timestamps. No personal or sensitive data is publicly stored.
