HBCE · EUROPEAN REALITY ENGINEERING LABORATORY · IDENTITY · GOVERNANCE · PUBLIC PROOF · VERIFICATION

Verifiable digital infrastructures for accountable operations

HBCE is a European laboratory for the design of digital infrastructures in which actions remain attributable, traceable, and reconstructible through time. The stack is organized around six public gateways: Spec, Activate, Joker-C2, Registry, Verify, and Pricing.

HBCE IN ONE SEQUENCE

Specification defines the rules. Activation establishes identity. Registry publishes minimized public proof. Verification reproduces deterministic validation. Joker-C2 governs execution. Pricing maps adoption across civil, business, industrial, and institutional scales.
