What HBCE is

HBCE Identity Primary Record (IPR) is not a profile system. It is an operational identity layer built to support auditability, minimization and deterministic verification across institutional and industrial contexts.

  • Append-only history (no rewrite)
  • Hash-only public proofs (GDPR-min, no public personal data custody)
  • Fail-closed validity (missing proof → INVALID)
  • EU-first positioning and governance mapping
  • Deterministic verification (PASS/FAIL, reproducible)
