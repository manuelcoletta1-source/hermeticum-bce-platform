What HBCE is

HBCE (Hermeticum European Cybernetic Bank) is an operational layer for persistent identity and verifiable proof. It is not a public personal-data database: the public layer is hash-only and the system runs in fail-closed mode.

IPR — Identity Layer
Persistent operational identity for people, AI, organizations, and critical systems.
Registry — Public Proof
Append-only registry: deterministic hash verification + timestamp integrity.
