Operating principles

  • UE-first: designed with EU compliance and auditability as the baseline.
  • Audit-first: evidence generation is a primary function, not an afterthought.
  • Fail-closed: if required proofs are missing or invalid → access/validation fails.
  • Hash-only: public layer uses minimized proofs (no public custody of personal data).
  • Append-only: registry and evidence are chained; tampering becomes detectable.
