How this system is structured

1 · Company (R&D)

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l.

The R&D entity building European-first protocols, verification flows, and deployment playbooks for operational identity. Engineered for GDPR-min and audit-first adoption.

2 · Infrastructure

HERMETICUM — European Cyber Bank

The infrastructure layer (not a financial bank): a bank-like system for digital execution. It issues and verifies identity proofs for modules, operator networks, and territorial nodes.

3 · Customer instrument

IPR (Identity Primary Record)

The customer’s operational instrument: a private Release file + a public hash-only proof. It enables deterministic access, accountability, and module activation.
