Research mission

Hermeticum is an independent European research and development laboratory focused on the next infrastructure layer of technological civilization: operational identity.

The laboratory develops identity, responsibility and governance systems for humans, artificial intelligence, robotics and critical infrastructures.

Core research areas

Operational Identity Infrastructure

Persistent identity layer for humans, AI and autonomous systems.

AI Governance Architecture

Verifiable and auditable AI integrated with operational identity.

Autonomous Systems Identity

Identity framework for robots, vehicles and intelligent infrastructures.

Preventive Responsibility Systems

Ex-ante authorization and audit infrastructure for critical actions.

European Infrastructure Stack

Federated model aligned with European technological and legal frameworks.

Active development

The laboratory is currently developing and testing:

  • IPR — Identity Primary Record infrastructure
  • Append-only identity registry
  • Operator network model
  • AI governance integration
  • Autonomous systems identity layer
  • European pilot node — Torino
