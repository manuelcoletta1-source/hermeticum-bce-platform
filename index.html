1. The Company

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. operates as an independent European R&D laboratory focused on identity infrastructure, operational verification systems, and deterministic audit frameworks.

HBCE develops verifiable digital identity architecture aligned with EU principles: data minimization, accountability, and operational resilience.

2. Hermeticum Banking Cyber Europe

Hermeticum Banking Cyber Europe (HBCE) represents a conceptual cyber-infrastructure model inspired by financial-grade traceability.

  • Identity as primary asset
  • State-based operational validation
  • Append-only event registry
  • Fail-closed verification logic

The objective is not banking services, but banking-level identity integrity applied to digital operations.

3. IPR — Identity Primary Record

IPR is the foundational operational instrument. It defines a persistent identity object with measurable state.

  • ACTIVE
  • FROZEN
  • REVOKED

IPR enables:

  • Deterministic verification (SHA-256 match)
  • Append-only public registry proof
  • Operator authorization layering
  • Lifecycle certification cycles

IPR is not an account. It is an operational identity structure.

4. AI JOKER-C2

AI JOKER-C2 is defined as an evolutionary cybernetic entity operating within the HBCE identity framework.

It is not autonomous sovereignty. It operates under root operator authority and policy constraints.

  • Protocol design assistance
  • Verification logic modeling
  • Resilience analysis
  • System architecture refinement

AI JOKER-C2 exists as an IPR-based operational AI system.
