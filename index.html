Foundational question

Before a system acts, who has the right to make it act?

Autonomous systems, AI agents, robots and digital infrastructures execute decisions every day. If execution cannot be attributed and verified after the event, legitimacy remains ambiguous.

Can a system execute without a verifiable operational identity?

If the answer is no, identity must exist before execution.
