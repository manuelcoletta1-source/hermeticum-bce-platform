HBCE — Operational Identity & Certified Events

HBCE is a layered European infrastructure for operational identity (IPR), certified events (IO-SPACE), integrity defense (CyberGlobal) and command coordination (JOKER-C2) across humans, AI, machines and critical systems. The network operates audit-first, fail-closed and hash-only public.

Operational principles

Audit-first
Critical actions produce verifiable proofs and timestamp integrity.
Fail-closed
If identity, license or verification fails, the system blocks by design.
Hash-only public
Public layer exposes cryptographic proofs only. No public custody of personal data.
