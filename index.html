Enterprise architecture (five pillars)

I · IDENTITY (IPR)

Persistent operational identity for AI nodes and human operators. Identity is referenced in every event.

II · EXECUTION CAPSULE

Canonical input/output, deterministic hashing, append-only chaining, and signature. The model remains external; the execution becomes attestable.

III · POLICY ENFORCEMENT

Fail-closed gatekeeping: if integrity, chain, or signature rules fail, the operation is denied.

IV · PUBLIC REGISTRY

Hash-only registry for public verification and audit evidence without exposing sensitive payloads.

V · FEDERATION READY

Provider-neutral governance layer that can wrap OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, and local/open-weight models under the same verification contract.
