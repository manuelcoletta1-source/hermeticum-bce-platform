Protocol posture

HBCE systems are built under hard design constraints. These are the conditions that make auditability and proof possible.

HASH_ONLY

Proof without public custody

Evidence is designed to remain verifiable while minimizing unnecessary public exposure.

APPEND_ONLY

Evidence can grow, not rewind

Operational history is extended through new events instead of retroactive rewriting.

FAIL_CLOSED

Uncertainty is not permission

If the system cannot deterministically prove validity, execution is blocked.

DATA MINIMIZATION

GDPR-min by design

Verification logic minimizes exposure and does not depend on public custody of personal data.

STATE SEPARATION

Identity is not authorization

Identity, permission, runtime state, and evidence remain structurally distinct.
