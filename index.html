Safety & compliance posture

HBCE is designed to minimize public data exposure and to maximize auditability. Public artifacts are hash-only. Validation is deterministic. Missing evidence results in INVALID. This is not a UX choice — it is an engineering constraint.

  • UE_FIRST and AUDIT_FIRST as default posture
  • FAIL_CLOSED execution: block on uncertainty
  • HASH_ONLY public surface: no public identity custody
  • APPEND_ONLY evidence chain: reproducible verification
