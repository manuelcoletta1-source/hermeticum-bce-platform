JOKER-C2 · DETERMINISTIC GOVERNANCE · EU-FIRST · AUDIT-FIRST · FAIL-CLOSED

Govern AI execution with deterministic proof

Joker-C2 is an execution governance layer that binds operations to IPR identities, enforces policy deterministically (PASS/FAIL/INVALID), and emits audit-ready receipts for every permitted action. It does not replace models — it governs what they are allowed to execute.

THE QUESTION NOBODY ASKED (BUT EVERYONE NEEDS)

When an AI system causes a real-world effect, who can prove — cryptographically and deterministically — who authorized it, under which policy, and why it was allowed (or blocked)?

Joker-C2 converts “AI output” into a governed operational event: identity → policy → PASS/FAIL → signed receipt → independent verification.

AUTHORIZATION

Identity-bound authority

Operations execute only inside an explicit scope bound to IPR identities (who/what/where/why).

ENFORCEMENT

Fail-closed by default

Default state is deny. If integrity, identity, or policy checks fail: BLOCK. No “maybe”.

EVIDENCE

Verifiable receipts

Each permitted execution emits a hash-chained receipt signed with Ed25519, verified as PASS/FAIL.

The gap Joker-C2 closes

Most AI stacks can generate content, but cannot deterministically prove governance when actions touch reality: who authorized an operation, under which policy, what evidence exists, and how to reproduce verification. Joker-C2 is the missing layer between generation and execution.

PROBLEM

Non-opposable actions

“It ran because the model said so” is not audit evidence.

SOLUTION

Deterministic gate

Policy checks produce a binary outcome: PASS or BLOCK.

OUTCOME

Proof trail

Hash-chained receipts + Ed25519 signatures enable independent verification.

Core protocol principles

Joker-C2 follows the HBCE protocol posture. These are not “nice to have”; they are the constraints that make governance real.

HASH_ONLY

Proof, not custody

Public evidence is hash-based. No personal data custody by design.

APPEND_ONLY

Immutable audit

Registries are append-only. Evidence can be added, never rewritten.

FAIL_CLOSED

Block on uncertainty

If the system cannot prove validity deterministically, it blocks execution.

DATA_MINIMIZATION

GDPR-min posture

Operational proofs minimize exposure. Verification does not require personal data.

STATE_SEPARATION

Clean boundaries

Identity primitives are separated from runtime state and execution context.
