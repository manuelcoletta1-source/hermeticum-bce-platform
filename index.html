What HBCE is

A standardized identity layer that enables verifiable operation across humans, organizations, AI agents, robots, drones, and autonomous systems.

  • EU-first / audit-first posture
  • Hash-only public surface (no identity custody)
  • Append-only evidence and deterministic verification
  • Fail-closed execution as a safety default

What HBCE is not

Not a marketing AI demo. Not a data-hungry identity platform. Not a system that “tries” to be safe. It is safe by default through failure behavior.

  • No biometric custody
  • No private-data public exposure
  • No “best effort” security—verification is deterministic
  • No execution when identity integrity fails
