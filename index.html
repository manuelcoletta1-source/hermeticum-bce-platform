Verifiable digital infrastructures for accountable operations

HBCE is a European research and engineering laboratory dedicated to the design, study, and construction of verifiable digital infrastructures. The laboratory focuses on systems where actions, decisions, and operational states remain reconstructible through time using deterministic evidence.

FOUNDATIONAL LOGIC

HBCE follows a simple operational sequence: Decision · Cost · Trace · Time. A configuration becomes observable when a decision is attributable, a cost introduces irreversibility, a trace remains reconstructible, and the configuration is exposed to time.

Operational principles

HBCE systems are built under hard constraints. These are not aesthetic slogans. They are the conditions that make verifiability real.

HASH-ONLY

Public proof minimization

Public registries expose proofs, not unnecessary personal data.

APPEND-ONLY

Chronological integrity

Events are added, never retroactively rewritten.

FAIL-CLOSED

Deterministic failure mode

If verification cannot be completed deterministically, the system defaults to failure.

HBCE produces technical protocols, infrastructure prototypes, metrological analysis, case-study datasets, and operational identity systems. All outputs are designed to be reconstructible and verifiable.
