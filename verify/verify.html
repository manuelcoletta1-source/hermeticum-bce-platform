VERIFY — public proof verification

This page performs deterministic public proof lookup against the HBCE public registry. The verification target is a payload_sha256 commitment.

A match means that a public proof record is present in the registry. It does not replace private evidence review, identity verification, contractual qualification, legal authorization, or institutional validation.

Policy: EU-first · audit-first · append-only · hash-only · fail-closed · GDPR-min · no public data custody
