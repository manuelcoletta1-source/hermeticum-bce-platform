Terms of Use

HBCE Platform is an experimental public gateway for research, documentation, technical verification, audit trails and fail-closed governance. The current scope is centered on IPR as the operational R&D/MVP product, IPR AI Audit Trail as the first demonstrable use case, HBCE as the operational ecosystem, JOKER-C2 as the runtime demonstrator and MATRIX as the architectural framework.

IPR Verified may use private identity verification based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. This private verification layer must remain separate from the public proof layer. The public registry must not contain tax codes, document numbers, identity documents or personal payloads.

Policy: EU-first · audit-first · private verification · hash-oriented evidence · append-only discipline · fail-closed · GDPR-min · no public data custody

1. Platform status

HBCE Platform is currently an experimental public research and development infrastructure. Some pages describe conceptual, prototype, demonstrative or planned components. Unless expressly stated otherwise, these components must not be interpreted as production controls, formal certifications, regulated services, qualified trust services or final operational guarantees.

The platform documents and tests models for operational identity, IPR-linked evidence, minimized public registry records, technical verification, private identity verification, EVT continuity, OPC proof and fail-closed governance.

IPR is not a public legal identity and does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries, digital signatures or qualified trust services. In the current phase, IPR is treated as an R&D/MVP operational record for verifiable events, proof material and workflows.

2. IPR profiles and verification boundary

IPR Base

IPR Base is a technical R&D record for testing, internal proof flows, hash-oriented attribution and controlled demonstrations. It does not create verified identity status.

IPR Verified

IPR Verified is the private verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence inside a controlled private verification workflow.

IPR Professional

IPR Professional is a future professional, business or audit workflow profile based on IPR Verified. It is intended for controlled pilot and R&D contexts, not automatic regulated certification.

Accepted valid identity-document categories for the target IPR Verified model are:

  • identity card / CIE;
  • passport;
  • driving license.

This evidence belongs only in the private verification layer. It must not be published in the public repository, public registry, public demo pages, public issues, public proof records or public audit artifacts.

3. System hierarchy

IPR

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is the base operational product. It is designed to connect an operational identity reference, an event context, a responsibility layer, proof material and a verification path.

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. It demonstrates how an AI-assisted document workflow can be connected to IPR, document hashing, governance decisioning, human validation, EVT, OPC and an audit-ready report.

HBCE

HBCE is the operational ecosystem containing policy logic, private verification boundaries, verification surfaces, registry references, evidence packs, event continuity, proof records and fail-closed rules.

JOKER-C2 and MATRIX

JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator used to show IPR in operation inside HBCE. MATRIX is the broader architectural framework. Neither should be presented as a public authority, regulated service or standalone legal certification system.

5. Private verification and no-custody rule

IPR Verified may require private identity evidence. This evidence must be handled only inside a private, authorized and controlled verification boundary. The public layer of the platform must use minimized records and hash-oriented references.

The public registry must not custody private evidence, tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, identity document scans, portrait images, customer data, credentials, secrets, private keys, private prompts, private outputs, production logs or sensitive operational payloads.

If verification requires private or sensitive material, that verification must take place outside the public layer through authorized, private channels that comply with applicable law and appropriate security controls.

7. Fail-closed

Fail-closed logic means that the platform must not produce implicit validity when a required condition is absent, ambiguous, unverifiable, inconsistent or malformed.

  • missing input → INVALID;
  • malformed hash → INVALID;
  • unreadable registry → NON_OPERATIONAL;
  • missing record → NO_PUBLIC_RECORD;
  • non-compliant schema → INVALID or NON_OPERATIONAL;
  • identity data in the public layer → block, removal or review;
  • sensitive data in the public layer → block, removal or review.

8. Permitted use

Permitted use of the public platform includes research, documentation, hash-oriented verification, defensive audit activity, modeling of minimized public registries, responsible AI governance, operational identity research and testing with owned, authorized, public or synthetic materials.

Private identity verification for IPR Verified must be handled only under an appropriate private legal, privacy, cybersecurity and operational boundary. The public platform must not be used as an identity-document upload surface, KYC repository or public identity custody layer.
