Public proof-reference registry

The Registry is the minimized public proof-reference surface for IPR Base records, IPR Verified proof references, EVT continuity references, OPC proof objects and audit-oriented evidence inside the HBCE ecosystem.

The registry may expose verification_level values such as IPR_BASE_NOT_VERIFIED or IPR_VERIFIED_PRIVATE_CHECK, but it must not expose tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portraits, addresses, dates of birth or personal payloads.

CURRENT PUBLIC BOUNDARY

Public records must remain minimized. The registry may expose identifiers, timestamps, event labels, node labels, status fields, verification level, hash references, EVT references, OPC references and verification metadata. It must not expose tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, private keys, credentials, internal endpoints, raw AI prompts, raw AI outputs, client files, personal data, customer data, production logs, confidential payloads or sensitive infrastructure topology.

IPR Verified relies on private verification. The public registry can record that a minimized proof reference exists, but it cannot certify legal identity, regulated KYC / AML compliance, public authority approval, eIDAS qualification or production authorization.

Public data minimization

Public identity, event or node labels may appear only when intentionally published for attribution, audit readability and registry continuity. Tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, confidential evidence, personal data, credentials, private keys, raw AI prompts, raw AI outputs and non-public payloads must not be stored in the public registry.

IPR evidence sequence

The registry supports IPR Base, IPR Verified and IPR AI Audit Trail by exposing minimized public references to private or controlled audit material. The preferred sequence is: 

PRIVATE_VERIFICATION_OR_IPR_BASE
→ VERIFICATION_LEVEL
→ IPR_OPERATIONAL_REFERENCE
→ HASH_ORIENTED_PROOF_REFERENCE
→ EVT_EVENT
→ OPC_PROOF
→ OPTIONAL_PUBLIC_REGISTRY_REFERENCE
→ VERIFY
→ FAIL_CLOSED_REVIEW

This sequence creates technical audit visibility without requiring public custody of tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, confidential prompts, outputs, review notes, evidence payloads, client files or regulated personal data.

Non-claims

  • The registry is not a public authority registry.
  • The registry is not a civil identity registry.
  • The registry is not a legal certification register.
  • The registry is not an eIDAS qualified trust service.
  • The registry is not a regulated KYC / AML registry.
  • The registry does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries or qualified trust services.
  • The registry does not produce automatic compliance, institutional validation or public-sector adoption.
  • The registry does not custody private evidence, tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, customer data, credentials or sensitive payloads.
