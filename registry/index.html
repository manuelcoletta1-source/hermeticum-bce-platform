Public R&D registry

The Registry is the minimized public visibility surface for the MATRIX AI Audit Trail MVP. It exposes hash-only and metadata-only records for R&D documentation, audit-trail review, evidence-pack continuity, and deterministic verification experiments.

This registry is not a public authority registry, not a regulated certification register, not an eIDAS qualified trust service, not a custodial identity service, and not a production compliance framework by itself.

CURRENT PUBLIC BOUNDARY

Public records must remain minimized. The registry may expose identifiers, timestamps, event labels, node labels, status fields, hash references, and verification metadata. It must not expose private keys, credentials, internal endpoints, raw AI prompts, raw AI outputs, client files, personal data, regulated identity documents, production logs, confidential payloads, or sensitive infrastructure topology.

Public identity or node labels may appear only when intentionally published for attribution, audit readability, and registry continuity. MATRIX audit sequence

The registry supports the MATRIX AI Audit Trail sequence by exposing public references to private audit material. The preferred sequence is: 

AI_OUTPUT_RECORDED
→ POLICY_CHECK
→ AI_OUTPUT_REVIEWED
→ HUMAN_VALIDATION
→ EVIDENCE_PACK_CREATED
→ VERIFICATION_RESULT
→ HASH_ONLY_PUBLIC_REFERENCE
→ FAIL_CLOSED_REVIEW

This sequence creates technical audit visibility without requiring public custody of confidential prompts, outputs, review notes, evidence payloads, client files, or regulated identity data.
