Operator reference receipt

Generate a privacy-minimal receipt for an operator reference public proof commitment. The receipt supports deterministic verification while keeping private evidence outside the public registry.

Do not enter real personal identifiers, emails, phone numbers, tax codes, identity documents, credentials, secrets, private keys, production logs, private evidence, raw AI prompts, raw AI outputs, client files, or sensitive operational payloads.
