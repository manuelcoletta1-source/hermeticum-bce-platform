Operator proof receipt

Generate a privacy-minimal receipt for an operator public proof commitment. The receipt is designed to support deterministic verification while keeping private evidence outside the public registry.

Do not enter real names, emails, phone numbers, tax codes, identity documents, credentials, secrets, private keys, production logs, private evidence, or sensitive operational payloads.

Flow
  • 1) Generate receipt.
  • 2) Use receipt_sha256 as public proof commitment.
  • 3) Append the minimized entry manually to registry/operators.json.
  • 4) Verify deterministically.
