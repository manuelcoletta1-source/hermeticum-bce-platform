HBCE Buyer-Facing Evidence One-Pager

BUYER-READABLE R&D BASELINE

Buyer summary

HBCE provides a public R&D evidence surface showing how governed digital-state evidence can be completed, indexed, explained and summarized without claiming certification, pilot readiness or external execution.

Current final audit marker: HBCE_EXECUTIVE_EVIDENCE_PACK_FINAL_AUDIT=1.

Who this is for

  • Banking risk teams
  • Compliance and audit teams
  • Security governance teams
  • Public-sector digital service owners
  • Innovation and procurement stakeholders

The problem

Digital decisions are often difficult to prove after the fact. Many systems preserve logs, but do not clearly distinguish accepted state, blocked state, invalidated evidence and non-authorized effects.

Regulated and public-sector workflows need evidence that can be read by technical, compliance and executive stakeholders.

What HBCE can show today

  • A completed MATRIX EG-001 T01-T37 evidence baseline
  • A Public Evidence Registry
  • A Public Evidence Explainer
  • An Executive Evidence Pack
  • Machine-readable JSON evidence artifacts
  • Git tags and GitHub Releases
  • Explicit no-dispatch and no-effect boundaries

Public evidence surfaces

  • HBCE Buyer-Facing Evidence One-Pager v001
  • HBCE MATRIX EG-001 T01-T37 Complete v001
  • HBCE Public Evidence Registry v001
  • HBCE Public Evidence Explainer v001
  • HBCE Executive Evidence Pack v001
  • Buyer Evidence One-Pager JSON
  • Buyer Evidence One-Pager documentation

Buyer value

  • A concrete starting point for evidence-based AI governance discussion
  • A public baseline for decision-proof, auditability and controlled digital-state transition
  • A safer pre-pilot conversation because boundaries and non-claims are explicit
  • A structure that can later support buyer-specific evidence packs

What this does not claim

This one-pager does not claim full MATRIX implementation, Level 1 pilot readiness, current C16 validity, current external validation acceptance, legal review, certification, commercial release authorization, current Level 4 eligibility, dispatch, target receipt, execution trace or effect evidence.

It does not authorize dispatch execution, does not emit a dispatch command, does not perform dispatch, does not call an external connector, does not contact a target system, does not create a target receipt, does not bind an execution trace and does not create effect evidence.

Recommended buyer next step

Evidence Review Session, 45-60 minutes.

  • Review the public HBCE evidence surface
  • Map the buyer's regulated workflow or decision process
  • Identify which evidence records would be required for a pilot candidate
  • Separate technical feasibility, legal review and commercial readiness

Output: pilot evidence requirements outline, not a pilot launch commitment.
