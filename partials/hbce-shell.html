PUBLIC PROOF LIMIT

No automatic legal certification

Public proof records indicate minimized proof commitments only. They do not replace private evidence review, legal authorization, institutional validation, regulated certification, contractual qualification, or civil identity verification.

FAIL-CLOSED RULE

Missing or invalid evidence must not produce validity

The fail-closed rule applies when public proof, schema validation, registry state, or required review conditions are missing, malformed, ambiguous, inconsistent, or unverifiable. 

missing input → INVALID
malformed hash → INVALID
unavailable registry → NON_OPERATIONAL
missing public record → NO_PUBLIC_RECORD
hash mismatch → HASH_MISMATCH / INVALID
sensitive public field → block or review
