## Purpose

This document defines the launch spine for the 2027-01-19 HBCE path, based on the current P003 Evidence Kernel line.

It translates the P003 technical work into controlled product language.

It does not establish production authorization, legal certification, L3 readiness, OPC ALLOW, PostgreSQL runtime readiness, public authority approval or deployment approval.

---

## Core launch sentence

HBCE is an operational trust layer for governed digital and AI actions, where identity, authorization, policy, payload, genealogy, revision and audit evidence are verified before action is accepted.

---

## Product spine

IPR → HBCE → JOKER-C2 → Evidence Kernel → Audit Value

IPR is the identity and responsibility record.

HBCE is the operational trust layer.

JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator and operational interface.

The Evidence Kernel is the verification layer for action acceptance.

Audit Value is the customer-facing value: traceable decisions, fail-closed controls, reconstructable evidence and controlled accountability.

---

## Controlled market framing

For banks, regulated companies and public-sector-adjacent workflows, the controlled framing is:

HBCE helps structure verifiable operational responsibility around digital and AI actions.

The platform direction is to connect:

- identity;
- authority;
- policy;
- action;
- revision;
- evidence;
- audit;
- responsibility.

The current P003 line is evidence-kernel R&D, not a production compliance product.

---

## Customer-facing value

The safe customer-facing value is:

- clearer audit trails;
- stronger operational accountability;
- fail-closed action acceptance;
- reconstructable decision evidence;
- separation between identity, authority, policy and action;
- controlled transition from R&D evidence to future runtime governance.

The unsafe customer-facing value is:

- legal certification;
- guaranteed compliance;
- production-ready regulated identity;
- autonomous authorization without human governance;
- verified L3 runtime deployment.

---

## Launch boundary

The launch path for 2027-01-19 should present HBCE as:

R&D evidence platform with a controlled operational trust architecture and a verifiable evidence-kernel line.

It should not present HBCE as:

Production-certified infrastructure or legally approved regulated identity provider.
