WHY THE ORIGINAL FORM IS REMOVED

Privacy and overclaim reduction

The previous STRONG form asked for email, phone, location-like metadata and reason-of-use fields, then generated a local pseudonymous HRR-style receipt.

Even though the original version attempted minimization by storing hashed contact signals, it could still be misread as an active onboarding or identity verification workflow. To avoid confusion with the current registry v3, payload_sha256 and no-public-data-custody model, the active form has been removed from this archive page.

Future strong-attribution workflows must be rebuilt under the current schemas, privacy rules, explicit legal boundaries, no-public-data-custody model and fail-closed verification semantics.
