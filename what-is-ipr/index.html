What is IPR?

IPR — Identity Primary Record is a verifiable operational identity record inside HBCE. It connects a subject, a role, an operation, an event, a proof reference, a responsibility boundary and a continuity trace.

IPR is not a simple login, not a generic account, not only a hash and not only a signature. It is the operational reference that allows HBCE to know who or what acted, under which role, on which event, with which proof, with which responsibility and with which continuity.

In the new HBCE access model, IPR is the base product. IPR Card is the verified operational access key. JOKER-C2 is the AI Operational Runtime accessed through verified IPR identity.

SIMPLE DEFINITION

IPR is operational identity plus proof continuity.

IPR is a stable operational reference for important digital actions. In ordinary systems, identity, access, logs, hashes, signatures and responsibility are often separated. IPR is designed to connect them into one reviewable operational record.

What IPR connects

IDENTITY

Who or what acts

IPR can reference a natural person, legal entity, operator, AI system, node, workflow or process.

ROLE

Under which capacity

The same subject can act under different roles: reviewer, issuer, operator, researcher, company, institution, runtime, node or process.

EVENT

What happened

IPR becomes useful when an upload, decision, validation, AI-assisted action, audit step or state transition must remain traceable.

PROOF

How it is checked

IPR can connect to hashes, timestamps, EVT records, OPC receipts, evidence packs and public proof references.

RESPONSIBILITY

Who owns the act

IPR preserves a responsibility context so that a process does not depend only on informal trust or scattered logs.

CONTINUITY

How the trace persists

IPR connects operations through events and proof references, preventing each action from becoming an isolated fragment.

WHAT IPR IS NOT

IPR must be understood precisely.

NOT OFFICIAL STATE IDENTITY
  • Not an identity card
  • Not a passport
  • Not CIE
  • Not SPID
  • Not an EUDI Wallet
  • Not a qualified eIDAS certificate
NOT A REGULATED SERVICE BY ITSELF
  • Not a public identity provider
  • Not a regulated KYC/AML provider
  • Not a qualified trust service
  • Not a banking service
  • Not legal certification by itself
  • Not public authority validation

Correct positioning: IPR is a verifiable operational identity reference inside HBCE. It may be connected to official European identity systems in future compliant integrations, but it does not replace them.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION / PUBLIC PROOF

Identity evidence stays private. Public proof stays minimized.

IPR separates private identity verification from public proof. Identity evidence may be used privately to create or verify an IPR, while the public layer exposes only minimized proof references.
