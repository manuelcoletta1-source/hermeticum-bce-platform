3. R&D rationale

Many organizations are beginning to use AI for document review, compliance support, cybersecurity documentation, procurement support, public-sector innovation, policy drafting and operational decision support.

The problem is that AI-assisted work is often not sufficiently traceable. Teams may lack a clear record of which document was analyzed, what prompt was used, what risk was assigned, who validated the output and which proof record exists.

IPR AI Audit Trail provides a small, testable answer to that problem: it converts AI-assisted document work into a controlled, attributable and audit-reportable workflow.

4. Pilot structure

Phase 1

Internal self-pilot

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. uses the MVP on its own governance, compliance, security and audit documents.

Phase 2

External 30-day pilot

One organization, one document workflow, 10–20 documents, 1–3 users, exported audit reports.

Phase 3

Hardening

Authentication, retention policy, persistent storage, access control, report verification and security review.

5. Funding logic

This page does not assert an awarded budget. Any budget discussion must be treated as a future scenario, not as an approved or active program.

A responsible funding path should start from small pilots and measurable outputs:

  • number of documents analyzed;
  • number of reports generated;
  • human validation completion rate;
  • clarity of audit reports;
  • risk classification usefulness;
  • EVT / OPC readability;
  • absence of secrets or sensitive data in public outputs.

Large-scale territorial node, institutional, dual-use, critical-infrastructure or EU-network scenarios are future research directions only. They require separate legal, security, privacy, procurement, institutional and funding review.
