# Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used within the Reality Engineering framework.

## Reality Engineering

A discipline focused on designing technological systems whose actions remain reconstructible, attributable, and verifiable over time.

## Corpus Esoterologia Ermetica

The conceptual and disciplinary foundation of the framework.  
It defines the language, axioms, and methodological principles of the system.

## Matrix Framework

An analytical model describing technological civilization as a system of infrastructures, actions, and coordination processes.

## HBCE Infrastructure

The operational infrastructure hosting the framework.  
HBCE provides the environment where identities, actions, and events are organized.

## Identity Primary Record (IPR)

A persistent identity layer used to associate actions with identifiable entities and record events within technological infrastructures.

## Joker-C2

A cybernetic coordination engine responsible for orchestrating actions across entities and infrastructures.

## Entity

An identifiable actor operating within the infrastructure.  
Entities may include humans, artificial intelligence systems, machines, or digital systems.

## Event

A recorded action associated with an entity and registered within the infrastructure.

## Infrastructure

The technological environment where entities interact and events are recorded.

## Esper-simento

A methodological concept combining experience and experiment to analyze operational configurations over time.
