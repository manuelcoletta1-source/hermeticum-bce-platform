# Joker-C2 Coordination Engine

Joker-C2 is the cybernetic coordination layer of the Reality Engineering framework.

The engine orchestrates actions performed by entities operating within the infrastructure.

Entities may include:

- human operators
- artificial intelligence systems
- autonomous machines
- digital infrastructures

## Purpose

The purpose of Joker-C2 is to coordinate operational activity across the system while maintaining traceability of actions.

## Functional Role

Joker-C2 acts as a coordination engine between:

entity  
↓  
action  
↓  
identity (IPR)  
↓  
event record  
↓  
infrastructure (HBCE)

This structure allows the reconstruction of decision processes over time.

## Operational Scope

The coordination engine can operate across different technological environments:

- digital infrastructure
- distributed systems
- autonomous operational environments

## Framework Position

Within the Reality Engineering architecture, Joker-C2 occupies the coordination layer between identity and infrastructure.
