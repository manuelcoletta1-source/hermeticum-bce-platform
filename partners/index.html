What HERMETICUM B.C.E. brings to discussion

PRODUCT

IPR — operational identity and proof record

IPR is the base operational product. It links subject, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility and continuity into an audit-ready reference model.

Open IPR
ACCESS KEY

IPR Card — operational access object

IPR Card represents operational identity state, verification level, activation EVT, OPC receipt and public proof reference. It is not a state identity document, SPID, EUDI Wallet or eIDAS qualified certificate.

Open IPR Card
FIRST MVP

IPR AI Audit Trail

The first MVP converts AI-assisted document work into an IPR-linked sequence: document hash, AI action, governance decision, human validation, EVT, OPC and audit-ready report.

Open MVP
RUNTIME

JOKER-C2 — governed AI runtime boundary

JOKER-C2 demonstrates governed AI runtime behavior. It does not bypass IPR state, policy, risk, human review, legal review, security review or operational scope.

Open JOKER-C2
