HBCE System Architecture

This page defines the structural hierarchy of the HBCE ecosystem. Not all repositories have the same operational weight.

Layer 0 — Root

hermeticum-bce-platform

Operational identity baseline. Standards. Registry. Deterministic verification layer.

Layer 1 — Identity Infrastructure

hermeticum-bce-ipr

Identity Primary Record logic.

ipr-core

Core identity computation and canonicalization.

ue-ipr-registry

EU-oriented identity registry architecture.

Layer 2 — AI & Execution

gitjoker-c2

Cybernetic decision layer.

ai-joker-workers

Execution workers.

hermeticum-bce-ai-workers

Industrial AI orchestration layer.

Layer 3 — Industrial / Governance

hermeticum-bce-b2b

Industrial adoption layer.

hermeticum-bce-b2g

Institutional adoption layer.

ipr-gov-eu-standard

Governance-grade profile.

Research / Experimental Layer

Research repositories exist as experimental domains and do not represent the operational baseline.

Principle

Root defines the standard. Other repositories extend capability. Not all components are baseline.
