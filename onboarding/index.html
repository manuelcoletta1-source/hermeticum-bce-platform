Verify who you are. Receive your IPR Card. Access governed AI.

IPR Onboarding Gateway is the operational access path of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. Platform. It is not a simple email-and-password registration page. It is the R&D model for registering, verifying and enabling a subject before access to governed AI workflows.

The target sequence is: registration, identity evidence, national tax identifier or national identification number, private verification package, provisional IPR state, review, IPR Card issuance, operational certificate and governed access to JOKER-C2.

This page describes the intended onboarding logic. It does not create regulated identity issuance, banking service, public authority validation, qualified eIDAS certification or production KYC/AML service.

WHY ONBOARDING EXISTS

JOKER-C2 must not start from a generic account

Classic AI access can start with an email, password and subscription. JOKER-C2 is designed differently. It starts from a verified operational identity reference. The user is not treated only as a generic account, but as an operational subject connected to role, responsibility, event continuity and proof references.

Onboarding sequence

STEP 01

Registration

The subject starts with basic account data: email, phone, password or passkey, country, language and subject type.

Subject types may include natural person, legal entity, operator, company, institution, professional or researcher.

STEP 02

Identity evidence

The private verification layer may request an accepted identity-document category such as identity card, CIE, passport, driving license or residence permit where applicable.

For European use, the neutral wording is national tax identifier / national identification number.

STEP 03

Verification package

A controlled evidence package may include document metadata, private review notes, selfie, short video or liveness check in future scoped implementations.

Sensitive identity material must remain private or controlled. It must not be published in the public registry.

STEP 04

IPR_PENDING

Until verification is completed, the record remains provisional. It can exist as a pending operational state, but it must not grant full governed runtime access.

STEP 05

Review and activation

A verified subject can move from IPR_PENDING to IPR_ACTIVE only after review, policy validation and controlled activation.

Activation can generate an EVT event and an OPC proof reference.

STEP 06

IPR Card and access

The user receives an IPR Card and an operational certificate reference. JOKER-C2 access is allowed only where the IPR state and policy conditions permit it.

STATE MACHINE

IPR is a stateful operational identity record

Onboarding must not be treated as a single static form. It is a state transition process. Each state has consequences for verification, access, audit and runtime permission. 

IPR_DRAFT

IPR_PENDING

ACCESS CONTROL

JOKER-C2 access depends on IPR state

The runtime access rule is fail-closed. Missing, provisional, suspended, revoked or expired identity states must not produce full runtime access.

Verification levels

L0

Technical R&D reference

Local or technical reference used for internal tests, demonstrations, hash-oriented attribution or non-production workflows.

L1

Basic account verification

Account and contact-level checks, such as email or phone verification. This does not by itself create document-verified operational identity.

L2

Document-based private verification

Private review of accepted identity-document evidence and controlled linkage to a national tax identifier or national identification number where applicable.

L3

Enhanced operational verification

Enhanced private verification with document evidence, national identifier reference, photo, short video, liveness or manual review in a separately scoped implementation.

IPR CARD ISSUANCE

The output of onboarding is an operational access key

After successful verification, the platform may issue an IPR Card. The IPR Card is the user-visible operational identity card inside HBCE. It can be represented as a PDF certificate, JSON credential, QR verification reference or digital badge in future controlled implementations.

PUBLIC / PRIVATE SEPARATION

Private identity evidence must not become public registry content

The onboarding model separates the private verification layer from the public proof layer. Public proof exists to verify the presence of a minimized proof reference, not to expose identity documents or personal evidence.

LEGAL AND REGULATORY BOUNDARY

IPR Card is operational, not official state identity

IPR Card is a digital operational card issued within the HERMETICUM B.C.E. environment. It does not replace an identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet or qualified eIDAS certificate, unless future integrations with recognized identity or trust-service systems are separately established.

HBCE does not issue official European identity. HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems in future compliant integrations.

Any real production onboarding, document verification, liveness detection, biometric comparison, customer identity workflow, enterprise deployment or institutional integration requires separate legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and operational review.
