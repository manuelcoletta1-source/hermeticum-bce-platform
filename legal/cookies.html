Cookie Policy

HBCE Platform is designed as a privacy-minimal public gateway. The public pages are intended for documentation, hash-only verification, minimized registry consultation, defensive audit, and research.

The platform does not intentionally use profiling cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioral tracking cookies.

Policy: privacy-minimal · no profiling cookies · no advertising cookies · no public identity custody · static public gateway

Legal overview Privacy Terms Contact

1. Cookie use

HBCE Platform does not intentionally use cookies for profiling, advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-site tracking, or user identity custody.

If a browser, hosting provider, embedded third-party service, or security layer creates technical cookies or similar technical signals, those are outside the direct application logic of this repository and should be interpreted according to the relevant provider’s policy.

2. Technical storage

Some browser-side tools in the platform may perform local computation, such as SHA-256 hashing, registry lookup, receipt verification, or evidence-pack validation.

These tools are designed to operate client-side and do not require profiling cookies. Local browser behavior, cache, file selection, or clipboard use may depend on the user’s browser settings.

3. No profiling or advertising

  • No intentional profiling cookies.
  • No intentional advertising cookies.
  • No intentional behavioral tracking cookies.
  • No intentional third-party marketing trackers.
  • No public identity-custody cookie layer.

4. Static hosting and provider logs

HBCE Platform may be served through static hosting, including GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP address, user agent, request time, and similar operational metadata according to their own policies.

This repository does not use provider logs as an identity registry, marketing profile, public proof registry, or user-tracking database.

5. Consent

Since this public gateway does not intentionally use profiling, advertising, or behavioral tracking cookies, a profiling-cookie consent banner is not implemented at this stage.

If future functionality introduces non-essential cookies, analytics, third-party tracking, or comparable technologies, this policy should be updated and the appropriate consent or configuration mechanism should be added where required.
