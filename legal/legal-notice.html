5. Fail-closed interpretation

The fail-closed principle requires that missing, ambiguous, unverifiable, malformed, or inconsistent states do not produce implicit validity.

  • missing input → INVALID;
  • malformed hash → INVALID;
  • unreadable registry → NON_OPERATIONAL;
  • missing record → NO_PUBLIC_RECORD;
  • hash mismatch → INVALID or HASH_MISMATCH;
  • sensitive data in the public layer → review, block, or removal.
