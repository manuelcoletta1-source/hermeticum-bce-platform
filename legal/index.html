Legal and policy boundary for HBCE Platform, IPR Card and JOKER-C2.

This page defines the legal and policy posture of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. public platform. The current architecture is organized around HBCE Platform as the IPR Onboarding Gateway, IPR as the first operational product, IPR Card as the verified operational access key, and JOKER-C2 as the AI Operational Runtime accessible through verified IPR identity.

This page is informational only. It is not legal advice, regulatory certification, fiscal advice, contractual advice, cybersecurity certification, public authority validation, production authorization or institutional approval.

The central boundary is simple: HBCE does not issue official European identity. HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems in future compliant integrations.

CORE LEGAL FORMULA

IPR Card is operational, not official state identity.

The following wording should remain stable across public pages, documentation, pilot materials and institutional communication: 

IPR Card is a digital operational card issued within the HERMETICUM B.C.E. environment.
It does not replace an identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet or qualified eIDAS certificate,
unless future integrations with recognized identity or trust-service systems are separately established.
HBCE does not issue official European identity.
HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems
in future compliant integrations.

This distinction protects the platform from overclaiming. The IPR Card is an internal operational object, not a public identity document and not a regulated identity instrument by itself.

PLATFORM POSITIONING

“Revolut-like” means onboarding experience, not banking activity.

HBCE Platform may be described as using an onboarding experience comparable in structure to modern fintech onboarding flows: registration, document evidence, verification status, card-like credential and controlled access. This comparison is only functional and experiential.

It does not mean that HBCE Platform is a bank, payment institution, electronic money institution, financial intermediary, regulated KYC provider, AML provider, official identity provider or qualified trust-service provider.

Product and system hierarchy

PLATFORM

HBCE Platform

HBCE Platform is the IPR Onboarding Gateway. It is designed to register, structure, verify and prepare operational identity references before governed AI access.

PRODUCT

IPR

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is the first operational product. It connects subject, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility, continuity and access state.

ACCESS OBJECT

IPR Card

IPR Card is the operational card derived from an IPR record. It represents verification level, status, issuer, activation EVT, OPC receipt and public proof reference.

RUNTIME

JOKER-C2

JOKER-C2 is the AI Operational Runtime and R&D demonstrator. It should not be presented as a generic chatbot or as an AI surface accessible through simple email-only registration.

ECOSYSTEM

HBCE

HBCE is the governance ecosystem that connects IPR, IPR Card, EVT, OPC, registry, verification, policy, risk and fail-closed access logic.

FRAMEWORK

MATRIX

MATRIX is the wider architectural framework. It is not the first commercial product and should not be confused with regulated infrastructure status.

PROJECT STATUS

R&D, MVP and pilot boundary

HBCE Platform is currently an experimental research and development public infrastructure. It documents and tests models for operational identity, private identity verification, IPR-linked evidence, audit-ready records, registry verification, EVT continuity, OPC proof, fail-closed governance and AI-assisted workflow traceability.

Unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active, the materials should be understood as R&D, MVP, prototype, conceptual, documentary or pilot-positioning material.

Any real pilot, customer test, institutional use, enterprise use or private identity-verification workflow requires a separate written scope, legal review, privacy review, cybersecurity review, contractual definition and operational validation.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION

Private verification must remain separate from public proof.

IPR Verified may use identity evidence only in a private, controlled verification layer. This private layer must be legally, technically and organizationally separated from the public proof layer.

Core principles

EU-FIRST

Policy reference posture

The European regulatory environment is treated as the default policy reference point for governance analysis. This does not mean the platform is certified, approved, endorsed or adopted by any EU institution, national authority, regulator, notified body or public administration.

AUDIT-FIRST

Reviewable records

Relevant records should remain reviewable, reconstructible and documented through audit traces, issue records, proof references, evidence packs, public documentation and append-only change history.

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

Missing, malformed, inconsistent, unverifiable or ambiguous states must not produce implicit validity. When a required condition is not satisfied, the workflow should return INVALID, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, NON_OPERATIONAL, REVIEW_REQUIRED or ACCESS_DENIED.

MINIMIZED PUBLIC PROOF

Hash-oriented exposure

The public layer should publish minimized proof references through hash-oriented commitments, timestamps, public labels and public status metadata. It must not publish identity files, secrets, credentials or sensitive operational payloads.

PUBLIC PROOF LIMIT

Public proof does not replace private validation.

A match in the public registry means that a public proof reference is present. It does not replace:

  • private evidence review;
  • identity verification;
  • tax-code or national identifier verification;
  • identity-document verification;
  • liveness or biometric review;
  • contractual qualification;
  • legal authorization;
  • institutional validation;
  • regulated certification;
  • KYC / AML due diligence;
  • tax or accounting documentation;
  • cybersecurity assurance;
  • public authority approval;
  • customer acceptance or production authorization;
  • JOKER-C2 access approval by itself.

Public verification should be read as technical proof-reference verification, not as automatic legal certification, regulated evidentiary status or official identity validation.

GDPR MINIMIZATION

Public pages must not become identity custody.

The platform is designed to minimize what is exposed publicly and to keep private evidence outside public channels. The public layer should demonstrate integrity and proof-reference presence without revealing private evidence, national identifiers, regulated identity material or sensitive personal data. 

public layer
→ hash-oriented reference

→ verification level

→ EVT / OPC references where appropriate
→ no identity document custody
→ no tax-code custody
