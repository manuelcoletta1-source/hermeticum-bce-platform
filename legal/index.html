Legal & policy overview

This page summarizes the legal and policy posture of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. public platform. The current platform is positioned around IPR as the base operational product, IPR Verified as the private-verification profile, IPR AI Audit Trail as the first MVP, HBCE as the operational ecosystem, JOKER-C2 as the runtime demonstrator, and MATRIX as the architectural framework.

This page is informational only. It is not legal advice, regulatory certification, fiscal advice, contractual advice, cybersecurity certification, public authority validation, production authorization or automatic institutional authorization.

1. Project status

HBCE Platform is currently an experimental research and development public infrastructure. It documents and tests models for operational identity, private identity verification, IPR-linked evidence, audit-ready records, registry verification, EVT continuity, OPC proof, fail-closed governance and AI-assisted workflow traceability.

Unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active, the materials should be understood as R&D, MVP, prototype, conceptual, documentary or pilot-positioning material.

The platform does not claim legal certification, regulatory approval, eIDAS qualification, qualified trust-service status, public-sector adoption, external audit certification, automated compliance, legally binding evidence status by itself, production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness, public authority approval, regulated KYC / AML provider status or financial authorization.

2. Product and system hierarchy

IPR — base operational product

IPR, or Identity Primary Record, is the base operational product. It is designed to connect an operational identity reference, event context, responsibility layer, proof material and verification path. It is not a legal identity scheme and does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries, digital signatures or qualified trust services.

IPR Verified — private verification profile

IPR Verified may use private identity verification based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. Accepted document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license. This evidence belongs only in the private verification layer and must not be published in the public registry.

IPR AI Audit Trail — first MVP

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first demonstrable MVP. It shows how AI-assisted document work can be connected to IPR, document hashing, governance decisioning, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof and audit-ready reporting.

HBCE — operational ecosystem

HBCE is the operational ecosystem around IPR. It includes governance logic, private verification boundaries, registry references, verification surfaces, evidence packs, EVT continuity, OPC proof and fail-closed rules.

JOKER-C2 and MATRIX

JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator used to show IPR in operation inside HBCE. MATRIX is the broader architectural framework. Neither JOKER-C2 nor MATRIX should be presented as the regulated product, public authority infrastructure or final commercial certification system.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. is the project’s R&D and operating vehicle for developing, testing and presenting the platform. Public material must avoid implying regulatory authorization, public-sector adoption, regulated identity-provider status, regulated KYC / AML provider status or public authority validation.

3. Private verification legal boundary

IPR Verified may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a private, controlled verification layer. This private layer must be legally, technically and organizationally separated from the public proof layer.

The private verification layer may include:

  • tax code or fiscal code;
  • identity card / CIE;
  • passport;
  • driving license;
  • private evidence package;
  • verification status generated from controlled evidence.

The public layer must not contain tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, addresses, birth dates, biometric material, private evidence or full civil identity payloads.

The legal posture is private verification with public proof minimization. It is not public identity custody.

4. Core principles

EU-first

The European regulatory environment is treated as the default policy reference point for governance analysis. This does not mean that the platform is certified, approved, endorsed or adopted by any EU institution, national authority, regulator, notified body or public administration.

Audit-first

Relevant records should remain reviewable, reconstructible and documented through audit traces, issue records, proof references, evidence packs, public documentation and append-only change history.

Fail-closed

Missing, malformed, inconsistent, unverifiable or ambiguous states must not produce implicit validity. When a required condition is not satisfied, the workflow should return INVALID, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, NON_OPERATIONAL or require private and manual review.

Hash-oriented and minimized

The public layer should publish minimized proof references, primarily through hash-oriented commitments, timestamps, public labels and public status metadata. The public layer should not contain tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, secrets, credentials, customer files or sensitive operational payloads.

5. Public proof does not replace private validation

A match in the public registry means that a public proof reference is present. It does not replace:

  • private evidence review;
  • identity verification;
  • tax-code verification;
  • identity-document verification;
  • contractual qualification;
  • legal authorization;
  • institutional validation;
  • regulated certification;
  • KYC / AML due diligence;
  • tax or accounting documentation;
  • cybersecurity assurance;
  • public authority approval;
  • customer acceptance or production authorization.

Public verification should be read as technical proof-reference verification, not as automatic legal certification, regulated evidentiary status or official identity validation.

6. GDPR minimization and data handling

The platform is designed to minimize what is exposed publicly and to keep private evidence outside public channels. The public layer should demonstrate integrity and proof-reference presence without revealing private evidence, tax-code data, regulated identity material or sensitive personal data.

Public layer

  • public proof references only;
  • payload_sha256 or equivalent hash-oriented commitment fields;
  • minimized labels such as subject_label and operator_label;
  • verification level, where appropriate;
  • timestamps and public lifecycle status;
  • EVT / OPC identifiers where appropriate;
  • no tax-code custody;
  • no document-number custody;
  • no private evidence custody;
  • no identity documents;
  • no credentials, secrets, tokens, passwords or private keys.

Private or operational layer

  • complete evidence should remain under holder or authorized-process control;
  • private verification should use controlled and lawful channels;
  • only the minimum necessary information should be shared;
  • sensitive material must not be placed in the public repository, public registry or public demo surfaces;
  • external pilots require a defined legal basis, data boundary, security boundary and responsibility owner.

Practical rule: never send or publish secrets, passwords, seed phrases, private keys, raw identity documents, tax codes, document numbers, authentication tokens, private evidence, customer files, confidential third-party material, production logs, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive operational payloads in public issues, public registry records, public pages or public commits.

7. No public identity custody

HBCE Platform does not position the public registry as a public identity-custody database. The registry is a public proof-reference layer for minimized technical commitments.

Practical interpretation

  • the public registry is not a civil registry;
  • the public registry does not replace official documents;
  • the public registry does not publish private identity evidence;
  • the public registry does not store tax codes, document numbers or identity documents;
  • the public registry does not perform regulated KYC, AML screening or official identity verification;
  • the public registry supports integrity and public-proof lookup through hash matching;
  • complete evidence remains outside the public layer;
  • private validation requires separate controlled processes.

8. Relationship with AI governance

IPR AI Audit Trail and JOKER-C2 are designed to support audit-oriented AI governance experiments. AI outputs must remain decision-support unless separately reviewed, approved and validated by the responsible human, professional, organization or institution.

The platform does not authorize AI systems to replace human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility. Any use in regulated or high-accountability contexts requires independent legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and operational assessment.

9. Defensive use and prohibited use

HBCE Platform should be used only for lawful, authorized, defensive, audit-oriented, research, documentation, verification and governance purposes.

The platform must not be used for unauthorized access, offensive cybersecurity activity, exploitation, malware development, credential theft, evasion, data exfiltration, destructive testing, unlawful surveillance, harmful automation or any activity that exceeds authorization.
