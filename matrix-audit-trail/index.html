1. What the demo shows

The demo shows how an AI-assisted document workflow can be converted into an audit-ready technical trace. It is designed to make a simple commercial use case visible: AI document analysis should not remain an untraceable chat interaction. 

Document upload
→ AI analysis
→ governance decision
→ human validation
→ EVT event
→ OPC proof
→ audit report

The system produces a readable report containing document hash, AI action, risk class, runtime decision, human validation, EVT identifier, OPC proof and final audit state.

4. Commercial interpretation

PROBLEM

AI work is often not traceable

Ordinary AI document analysis may produce useful output, but organizations often cannot prove what happened, who validated it, or whether a final audit trail exists.

SOLUTION

Governed AI workflow

MATRIX AI Audit Trail connects AI-assisted document analysis to document hash, governance decision, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof and audit-ready report.

VALUE

Audit-ready output

The result is a readable report that a compliance consultant, cybersecurity reviewer, AI governance advisor, or internal audit team can inspect and export.

5. Correct use

The demo should be used only with synthetic, public or authorized documents. Do not upload secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, customer data, confidential third-party data, production logs, private evidence or sensitive operational payloads.

The MVP is intended for internal R&D testing, product demonstration and future pilot preparation.
