2. Why this research direction matters

When an autonomous or semi-autonomous system acts, the key governance questions are:

  • who or what is responsible for the action;
  • which identity or operational context is linked to the action;
  • what proof exists after the action;
  • whether the proof can be reconstructed later;
  • whether a human validation or oversight boundary exists;
  • whether missing evidence produces a fail-closed outcome.

These are research questions. They are not deployment claims.
