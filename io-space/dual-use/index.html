2. Defensive dual-use meaning

In this repository, dual-use means that the same governance principles may be studied across civil, industrial and institutional contexts without changing the safety posture.

The common posture is:

  • human responsibility;
  • audit-first documentation;
  • hash-only public proof;
  • append-only evidence logic;
  • fail-closed verification;
  • no public custody of private evidence or sensitive operational data;
  • defensive, authorized and non-offensive use only.

8. Recommended pilot boundary

Any security-relevant or public-sector-relevant pilot should start with non-sensitive documents and a narrow workflow.

  • duration: 30 days;
  • users: 1–3 authorized operators;
  • documents: 10–20 public, synthetic or explicitly authorized documents;
  • workflow: one AI-assisted document review process;
  • output: audit-ready reports;
  • review: traceability, human validation, report clarity and risk classification.

Any pilot involving real sensitive infrastructure, regulated data, cybersecurity incidents, personal data or confidential operational material requires prior legal, security, privacy and institutional review.
