1. What HBCE is

HBCE is the operational ecosystem used to organize identity-linked events, proof references, verification flows and audit-ready continuity. Its role is to make IPR usable inside controlled technical workflows rather than leaving operational identity as an abstract concept.

HBCE provides the surrounding structure around IPR: governance rules, public proof-reference surfaces, registry entries, verification states, EVT event continuity, OPC proof records, evidence packs, policy boundaries and fail-closed behavior.

HBCE should not be presented as the first product to sell. The first product is IPR. HBCE is the ecosystem that makes IPR operational, verifiable and demonstrable.

3. How HBCE supports IPR

HBCE supports IPR by connecting creation, evidence generation, event continuity, proof references, registry publication and verification into one reconstructable workflow. 

Create IPR
→ generate evidence material
→ record EVT continuity
→ generate OPC proof
→ expose minimized registry reference
→ verify evidence
→ produce audit-ready reconstruction
→ fail closed if required evidence is missing

This makes IPR usable in practical settings such as AI-assisted document review, governance reporting, internal audit support, compliance documentation, cybersecurity policy review and controlled R&D pilots.

5. Governance posture

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

If required evidence, registry state, schema validity, human validation or proof continuity is missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable, the workflow should block, deny, escalate or require manual review.

NO PUBLIC DATA CUSTODY

Minimized public layer

The public layer must not store identity documents, customer files, secrets, private keys, credentials, private prompts, private outputs, production logs or sensitive operational payloads.

AUDIT-FIRST

Reviewable continuity

Records, changes, proof references, findings and verification outputs should remain reconstructable through explicit technical artifacts.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

AI remains decision-support

HBCE does not authorize AI systems to replace human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility. Human validation remains part of the governance boundary.

6. What HBCE is not

  • HBCE is not the base commercial product. The base product is IPR.
  • HBCE is not a public authority infrastructure.
  • HBCE is not a civil identity registry.
  • HBCE is not a legal certification system.
  • HBCE is not an eIDAS qualified trust service.
  • HBCE does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, public registries or qualified trust services.
  • HBCE does not create automatic compliance with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law or sector-specific regulation.
  • HBCE does not replace external audit, legal review, cybersecurity review, institutional validation or production governance.
