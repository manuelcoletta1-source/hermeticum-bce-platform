WHAT HBCE IS

HBCE is the operating governance layer around IPR.

HBCE organizes identity-linked events, proof references, verification flows, access decisions and audit-ready continuity. Its role is to make IPR operational inside controlled technical workflows rather than leaving operational identity as an abstract concept.

HBCE provides the surrounding structure around IPR: onboarding boundaries, IPR Card status, governance rules, public proof-reference surfaces, registry entries, verification states, EVT event continuity, OPC proof records, evidence packs, policy boundaries, risk boundaries and fail-closed behavior.

HBCE should not be presented as the first product to sell. The first product is IPR. HBCE is the ecosystem that makes IPR operational, verifiable, governable and demonstrable.

Governance posture

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

If required evidence, registry state, schema validity, human validation, risk state or proof continuity is missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable, the workflow should block, deny, escalate or require review.

NO PUBLIC DATA CUSTODY

Minimized public layer

The public layer must not store identity documents, customer files, secrets, private keys, credentials, private prompts, private outputs, production logs, tax codes, national identifiers or sensitive payloads.

AUDIT-FIRST

Reviewable continuity

Records, changes, proof references, findings and verification outputs should remain reconstructible through explicit technical artifacts.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

AI remains decision-support

HBCE does not authorize AI systems to replace human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility. Human validation remains part of the governance boundary.

JOKER-C2 ACCESS GOVERNANCE

JOKER-C2 access depends on HBCE governance.

JOKER-C2 is not a generic AI runtime accessed only by email and subscription. Its governed access model depends on IPR state, IPR Card status, verification level, policy, risk, proof, scope and review. 

NO_IPR
→ ACCESS_DENIED

IPR_PENDING
→ REVIEW_REQUIRED

IPR_ACTIVE + policy pass + acceptable risk + required proof
→ access may be allowed

IPR_SUSPENDED / REVOKED / EXPIRED
→ ACCESS_DENIED / RENEWAL_REQUIRED

Access does not mean certification. Runtime permission does not replace legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance, procurement or institutional review.

BOUNDARY LAYER

Legal, Privacy and Claims define what HBCE does not claim.

HBCE requires a boundary layer because public proof, operational identity and governed runtime access can be misread as official certification if the limits are not explicit.

WHAT HBCE IS NOT
