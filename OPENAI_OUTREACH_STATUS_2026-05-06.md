## 2. Project positioning

The current project positioning is:

```text
IPR AI Audit Trail is the first concrete MVP use case built around IPR, the base operational product of the HBCE ecosystem.
```

IPR AI Audit Trail is an R&D-stage workflow for verifiable AI governance and auditability in regulated or high-accountability document processes.

It is designed to show how AI-assisted document work can be linked to:

- operational identity through IPR;
- optional private verification through IPR Verified;
- document hashing;
- governance decisioning;
- human validation;
- EVT event continuity;
- OPC operational proof;
- audit-ready reporting;
- fail-closed verification.

The project does not propose replacing human or institutional responsibility with AI automation.

The project proposes a governance perimeter where AI-assisted actions remain linked to human validation, responsibility ownership, policy constraints, auditability, and verifiable continuity.
