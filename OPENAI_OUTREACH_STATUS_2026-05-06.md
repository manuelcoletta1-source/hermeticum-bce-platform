## 2. Project positioning

MATRIX / HBCE is positioned as an experimental R&D framework for regulated AI governance and verifiable technological infrastructure.

Primary research areas:

- verifiable AI governance;
- operational identity;
- responsibility-preserving AI workflows;
- human validation;
- event continuity;
- fail-closed governance;
- audit-ready evidence records;
- defensive cybersecurity governance;
- regulated institutional decision-support;
- public-sector and critical-infrastructure AI governance.

The project does not propose replacing human or institutional responsibility with AI automation.

The project proposes a governance perimeter where AI-assisted actions remain linked to human validation, responsibility ownership, policy constraints, auditability, and verifiable continuity.
