3. What to include in a first message

A useful first message should be concise and non-sensitive. It should describe the intended discussion without attaching confidential material or identity evidence.

  • Request type: IPR Verified pilot, IPR AI Audit Trail demo, R&D pilot, incubation review, partner discussion, B2B/B2G scoping or technical due diligence.
  • Target use case: IPR, IPR Verified, IPR AI Audit Trail, AI governance, audit trail, compliance documentation or cybersecurity documentation.
  • Organization type: consultant, startup, enterprise, public-sector innovation unit, research group or institutional stakeholder.
  • IPR profile: IPR Base, IPR Verified or IPR Professional, if already clear.
  • Data boundary: synthetic, public, owned or authorized non-sensitive materials only.
  • Expected output: demo, pilot brief, technical review, roadmap, evidence model or partner evaluation.
  • Time horizon: exploratory, 30-day pilot, technical review or later-stage scoping.

For IPR Verified discussions, mention that private verification is in scope, but do not send the actual tax code, document number, identity document, scan, image or private evidence in the first message.
