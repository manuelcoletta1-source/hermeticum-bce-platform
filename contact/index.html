FIRST MESSAGE

What to include in a first message

A useful first message should be concise and non-sensitive. It should describe the intended discussion without attaching confidential material or identity evidence.

  • Request type: IPR onboarding, IPR Verified pilot, IPR Card scope, IPR AI Audit Trail demo, JOKER-C2 boundary, R&D pilot, incubation review, partner discussion, B2B/B2G scoping or technical due diligence.
  • Subject type: natural person, operator, company, institution, professional, researcher, legal entity or other.
  • Target use case: AI governance, audit trail, compliance documentation, cybersecurity documentation, internal review or public-sector documentation.
  • Organization type: consultant, startup, enterprise, public-sector innovation unit, research group or institutional stakeholder.
  • IPR profile: IPR Base, IPR Verified, IPR Card scope or IPR Professional, if already clear.
  • Data boundary: synthetic, public, owned or authorized non-sensitive materials only.
  • Expected output: demo, pilot brief, technical review, roadmap, evidence model or partner evaluation.
  • Time horizon: exploratory, 30-day pilot, technical review or later-stage scoping.

For IPR Verified discussion, mention that private verification is in scope, but do not send the actual tax code, national identifier, document number, identity document, scan, image, video or private evidence in the first message.
