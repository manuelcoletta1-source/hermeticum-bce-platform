HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. is a European deep-tech lab building persistent operational identity infrastructure for humans, artificial intelligence and autonomous systems.

Context

Operational AI, autonomous robots and cyber-physical systems increasingly act continuously over time. Current digital identity models are fragmented across platforms, updates and environments. A new layer is needed: persistent operational identity.

Mission

Build a European technical layer for continuity and verification: no centralized control, no surveillance — just verifiable operational continuity.

Core technology: Identity Primary Record (IPR)

  • Persistent (platform-independent)
  • Verifiable (without exposing sensitive data)
  • Evolutive (continuity across versions)
  • Derivable (operational identities for connected AI/robots/systems)

Domains

  • AI governance — identity for persistent AI agents
  • Robotics — continuity for autonomous machines
  • Critical infrastructure — energy, smart cities, complex systems
  • Space — identity for distributed/remote environments
