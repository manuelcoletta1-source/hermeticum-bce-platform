1. Enterprise problem

Enterprise teams are increasingly using AI to summarize, classify and review documents. However, ordinary AI usage is often not sufficiently auditable.

A compliance, cybersecurity, legal operations or internal audit team may need to prove:

  • which document was analyzed;
  • which AI action was requested;
  • which governance decision was applied;
  • whether human validation occurred;
  • which event and proof records were generated;
  • whether a final audit report exists.

MATRIX AI Audit Trail addresses this gap by turning AI document work into an audit-ready technical trace.

The MVP is deliberately narrow. It proves one concrete workflow before moving toward broader productization.

5. 30-day pilot model

A possible external B2B pilot should remain small and testable:

  • duration: 30 days;
  • users: 1–3 authorized operators;
  • documents: 10–20 synthetic, public or explicitly authorized documents;
  • workflow: one AI-assisted document analysis process;
  • output: audit-ready report package;
  • review: usability, traceability, report clarity, validation workflow and governance value.

Any pilot involving confidential, personal, regulated, client or sensitive operational data requires prior legal, privacy and security review.

6. What the MVP is not

  • It is not production-grade enterprise SaaS yet.
  • It is not legal certification.
  • It is not regulated compliance automation.
  • It is not external audit certification.
  • It is not public-sector adoption.
  • It is not eIDAS or qualified trust service functionality.
  • It does not replace legal, security, privacy or institutional review.
