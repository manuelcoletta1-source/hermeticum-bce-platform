1. What “Create IPR” means

Creating an IPR means preparing an operational identity record that can later be connected to hash-oriented evidence, event records, proof receipts and verification outputs. The purpose is not to replace legal identity systems, digital signatures or public registries. The purpose is to give HBCE a stable operational reference for traceable digital execution.

In practical terms, an IPR creation flow should produce or prepare a private operational record, compute proof material, connect it to evidence references and make it possible to verify whether the record still matches the available evidence.

2. Operational flow

The creation process should remain simple and reconstructable. An IPR record is created first, proof material is generated next, evidence is registered or referenced, and the record is verified before it is presented as operationally valid. 

Create IPR record
→ generate hash-oriented proof material
→ register or reference evidence
→ verify record and proof
→ use IPR in HBCE workflows

If the proof is missing, inconsistent or unverifiable, the system should not present the record as valid.

5. What this page does not claim

This creation gateway must be read within the current R&D/MVP boundary. It supports technical creation, proof generation and verification workflows, but it does not claim to issue official identity, replace legal identity systems or operate as a regulated certification authority.

  • It does not create a public legal identity.
  • It does not replace SPID, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, digital signatures or public registries.
  • It does not provide legal certification or regulated trust-service status by itself.
  • It does not make a record legally binding without external legal, contractual or institutional validation.
  • It does not authorize production use with secrets, private keys, credentials or sensitive third-party data.

Use only synthetic, public or authorized materials in demo and pilot contexts.
