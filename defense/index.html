2. Defensive security model

HASH-ONLY

Minimized public proof

Public evidence should use minimized proof commitments such as payload_sha256, not private evidence or sensitive operational data.

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

Missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable states must not produce authorization or validity.

AUDIT-FIRST

Reviewable workflow

Relevant document, AI, governance, human validation and proof events should remain reviewable.

7. Recommended pilot path

For security-relevant validation, begin with non-sensitive documents and a narrow workflow.

  • duration: 30 days;
  • users: 1–3 authorized operators;
  • documents: 10–20 public, synthetic or explicitly authorized documents;
  • workflow: one AI-assisted document review process;
  • output: audit-ready reports;
  • review: traceability, human validation, report clarity and risk classification.

Any pilot involving real sensitive infrastructure, regulated data, cybersecurity incidents, personal data or confidential operational material requires prior legal, security, privacy and institutional review.
