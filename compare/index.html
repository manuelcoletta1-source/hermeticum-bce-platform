Legacy logging vs deterministic evidence

This is a structural comparison. No marketing claims. The difference is reproducibility.

Structural comparison

Legacy logging

  • Editable or replaceable logs
  • Trust depends on platform control
  • Audits become narrative
  • Identity is session-based
  • AI instances are replaceable
  • Failure degrades silently

HBCE baseline

  • Append-only evidence trajectory
  • Verification independent of platform trust
  • Audits become deterministic
  • Identity is persistent trajectory
  • AI/machine bound to identity proofs
  • Failure is fail-closed (blocked)

What changes operationally

  • Incident response: less ambiguity, faster root cause work.
  • Liability: responsibility continuity becomes auditable.
  • Certification: evidence becomes portable and reproducible.
  • Autonomy: decisions stay admissible only if reproducible.

Rule: if a decision cannot be reproduced independently, it is not admissible for execution.
