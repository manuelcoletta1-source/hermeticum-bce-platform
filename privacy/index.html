PRIVACY · IPR ONBOARDING · IPR CARD · PRIVATE VERIFICATION · PUBLIC PROOF MINIMIZATION

Private identity evidence stays private. Public proof stays minimized.

HBCE Platform is designed around data minimization, private identity verification, local-first evidence handling, hash-oriented public verification and fail-closed registry semantics.

The current architecture is organized around HBCE Platform as the IPR Onboarding Gateway, IPR as the first operational product, IPR Card as the verified operational access key, and JOKER-C2 as the AI Operational Runtime connected to verified IPR identity.

IPR onboarding may require private or controlled identity evidence. The public layer must never become a custody layer for tax codes, national identifiers, document numbers, identity documents, selfies, liveness videos, private evidence, customer data, credentials, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive operational payloads.

IPR PRIVACY BOUNDARY

IPR is operational identity, not public identity custody.

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is an operational identity and proof record inside HBCE. IPR Base is a technical R&D profile and does not claim verified identity status. IPR Verified may require private identity verification. IPR Card represents an operational access state derived from the IPR record.

IPR is not a public identity document, not a civil registry entry, not a regulated KYC / AML service, not a public data vault and not a replacement for SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries, digital signatures or qualified trust services.

An IPR-linked workflow may expose a public proof reference to support later verification, but the complete identity material, tax code, document number, identity scan, private evidence, legal document, customer file and sensitive payload must remain outside the public repository and outside public registry surfaces.

PUBLIC REGISTRY DATA

The registry exposes proof presence, not private identity.

The public registry should contain only minimized public proof records. It may use fields such as:

  • ipr_id or public IPR reference
  • ipr_card_id or public IPR Card reference
  • subject_label or non-sensitive public label
  • operator_label where applicable
  • record_scope where needed
  • verification_level
  • status or ipr_status
  • payload_sha256
  • evt_id or activation EVT reference
  • opc_id or OPC receipt reference
  • timestamp
  • short non-sensitive public notes where needed

A public proof record only means that a minimized proof reference is present. It does not verify the private identity file, does not create official identity and does not authorize JOKER-C2 access by itself.

LOCAL-FIRST EVIDENCE

Full evidence should remain outside the public layer.

Full evidence files, identity materials, tax-code evidence, identity-document evidence, receipt preimages, private proofs, private documents, customer files and sensitive operational records should remain outside the public repository and outside the public registry.

The public layer may publish a hash-oriented commitment or minimized proof reference, but the complete evidence should remain under the control of the holder, maintainer, authorized organization or lawful private review process.

Any external pilot, customer workflow or production environment must define a separate legal basis, data boundary, security boundary, retention model, access-control model and responsibility owner.

DEMO AND PILOT BOUNDARY

Use only synthetic, public or authorized materials in demos.

Public demos and R&D pilot tests should use only synthetic, public or explicitly authorized materials. Do not upload or paste secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, tax codes, national identifiers, customer personal data, confidential third-party data, protected business material, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive evidence into public demo surfaces.

If a real deployment requires identity verification, tax-code checks, document checks, liveness checks or sensitive data, the public R&D setup is not sufficient by itself. A dedicated legal, privacy, security and operational environment is required before processing such data.

VERIFICATION

Verification checks public proof references.

Verification is performed through deterministic comparison against public proof references, primarily using payload_sha256, registry semantics and local hash comparison. 

PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT
→ valid public reference match exists

NO_PUBLIC_RECORD
→ no matching public proof record was found

INVALID
→ input is missing, malformed, forbidden or rejected

NON_OPERATIONAL
→ registry or verifier is unavailable, malformed or inconsistent

A public match does not replace private evidence review, identity verification, contractual qualification, legal authorization, regulated certification, cybersecurity assurance, institutional validation, JOKER-C2 access authorization or production approval.

COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The public HBCE Platform should avoid unnecessary tracking and unnecessary analytics. If analytics, cookies or third-party services are introduced in the future, they should be documented clearly and configured consistently with data minimization principles.

Public GitHub Pages hosting may generate standard technical logs under the hosting provider’s own policies. This repository does not use those logs as an identity-custody system and does not treat them as IPR evidence.

SECURITY REPORTS

Security reports and exposed data

If secrets, credentials, personal data, tax codes, national identifiers, identity documents, private evidence, customer material or sensitive operational payloads are discovered in the public repository, public registry or public pages, they should be reported according to SECURITY.md.

Do not copy, redistribute, exploit, index, reuse or amplify exposed sensitive material.
