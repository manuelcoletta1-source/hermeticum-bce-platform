1. The thesis (binary)

  • Autonomy requires accountability.
  • Accountability requires reproducible verification.
  • Reproducible verification requires deterministic identity proofs.
  • Deterministic identity proofs require append-only evidence and explicit authority.

When governance is real, validity must be PASS/FAIL. Missing proof is not “unknown”. It is INVALID (fail-closed).
