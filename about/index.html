What it is (and what it is not)

HERMETICUM is not a financial bank. It is a bank of operational identity and continuity over time: an infrastructure that binds systems to verifiable identity states, policies, and append-only trace.

Technology Foundations

HERMETICUM is built on three operational pillars: IPR, Joker-C2, and a node network.

IPR (Identity Primary Record)

Persistent operational identity. It is the minimum requirement to operate in the ecosystem. Public surface is hash-only. Evolution is append-only. Verification is deterministic.

Joker-C2 (cybernetic coordination layer)

A coordination layer for human–AI continuity. It integrates modules, assists operators and nodes, and enforces operational discipline through deterministic checks and audit constraints.

Node Network

Territorial and institutional nodes for EU-scale coordination. The network grows with active IPR density and certified operators, while preserving UE-first and audit-first constraints.

Pre-Execution Identity (the critical layer)

Traditional systems validate identity after execution (logs, incident response, forensics). HERMETICUM enforces identity before execution: if verification fails, execution is blocked.

Fail-Closed Rule

If a proof cannot be deterministically validated against the public registry, the operational state is NON-OPERATIONAL. No registry match means no permission to operate.
