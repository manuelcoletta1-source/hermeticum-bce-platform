About HERMETICUM B.C.E.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. is the R&D and operating vehicle behind the HBCE Platform: an IPR-first research infrastructure for operational identity, private verification, public proof minimization, audit-ready evidence chains and responsible AI governance.

The current product focus is IPR — Identity Primary Record. IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. HBCE is the operational ecosystem. JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator. MATRiX is the architectural framework.

Status: R&D platform · IPR-first · private verification · public proof minimization · not regulated certification

3. IPR Verified boundary

IPR Verified may use private identity verification based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. Accepted document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license.

This material belongs only in the private verification layer. The public layer must not store tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portrait images, biometric material, private evidence, personal data or customer payloads. 

PRIVATE VERIFICATION
→ IPR VERIFIED
→ HASH-ORIENTED PUBLIC PROOF REFERENCE
→ REGISTRY / VERIFY
→ AUDIT-READY WORKFLOW

IPR Verified does not create legal identity, regulated KYC / AML compliance, eIDAS qualification, public authority approval or production authorization by itself.

4. What HBCE studies

Operational identity

How actions can remain connected to an operational identity reference over time.

Audit-ready AI workflows

How AI-assisted document work can remain traceable, human-validated and reconstructible.

Public proof minimization

How public systems can verify proof references without exposing identity evidence or sensitive payloads.

Fail-closed governance

How systems should block, escalate or require review when proof is missing, malformed or inconsistent.

EVT / OPC continuity

How events and operational proof references can support audit-ready reconstruction.

B2B / B2G pilot design

How controlled pilots can test compliance, cybersecurity, AI governance and institutional documentation workflows.
