ABOUT · HBCE PLATFORM · IPR ONBOARDING · IPR CARD · JOKER-C2 · PUBLIC PROOF

HBCE Platform is the IPR Onboarding Gateway for governed AI access.

HBCE Platform connects operational identity, proof, audit and runtime governance. It is not a simple login page where a user enters an email and starts using an AI model. It is an R&D platform designed to explore how verified operational identity can become the access threshold for governed AI workflows.

The current architecture is simple: Platform is the onboarding gateway, IPR is the first product, IPR Card is the operational access key, JOKER-C2 is the governed AI runtime, IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP, Registry / Verify is the public proof layer, and Legal / Privacy / Claims defines the boundary layer.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. develops and presents this platform as an experimental R&D infrastructure. It does not claim official identity issuance, public authority status, banking authorization, eIDAS qualification, regulated KYC / AML provider status or production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness by itself.

WHY IT EXISTS

AI needs operational identity, not only accounts.

Modern AI systems can generate text, analysis, decisions and reports, but the surrounding operational structure is often weak. A normal account can say who logged in, and a log can say something happened, but those elements alone do not create a complete operational chain.

HBCE studies how to connect identity, role, action, event, proof, responsibility and continuity so that AI-assisted workflows can remain attributable, reviewable and reconstructible.

IPR VERIFIED AND IPR CARD BOUNDARY

Private verification stays private. Public proof stays minimized.

IPR Verified may use identity evidence and national identifier evidence only in a controlled private verification layer. IPR Card can represent the operational result of an IPR state inside HBCE, but it is not official identity.

The public layer must not store tax codes, national identifiers, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portrait images, liveness videos, biometric material, private evidence, personal data or customer payloads.

OPERATIONAL POSTURE

EU-first, audit-first, fail-closed.

HBCE Platform follows a governance posture based on EU-first analysis, audit-first documentation, fail-closed interpretation, append-only public proof discipline, private verification, public proof minimization and no public data custody.

This posture is an R&D and governance design direction. It is not a formal certification, regulatory approval, public authority validation, eIDAS qualification, KYC / AML provider status, cybersecurity certification or production compliance claim.
