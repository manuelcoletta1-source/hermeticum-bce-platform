Position in the HBCE stack

Joker-C2 is the runtime layer where AI-assisted interaction becomes governed. In the HBCE architecture: IPR provides operational identity, Joker-C2 governs AI runtime execution, EVT records operational events, OPC creates operational proof records, Registry publishes minimized public proof, and Verify reproduces validation deterministically.

MATRIX AI Audit Trail flow

MATRIX AI Audit Trail is the first concrete MVP extracted from the MATRIX / HBCE / AI JOKER-C2 architecture. It turns AI-assisted document analysis into an audit-ready workflow.

1 · DOCUMENT

Upload and hash

A document is read locally and assigned a technical hash commitment.

2 · AI

Analyze

AI output is treated as decision-support, not as autonomous final authority.

3 · HUMAN

Validate

Human approval, correction or rejection creates the responsibility boundary.

4 · PROOF

Report

EVT, OPC and an audit-ready report are generated after validation.
