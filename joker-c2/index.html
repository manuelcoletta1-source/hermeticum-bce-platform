JOKER-C2 · RUNTIME PROTOTYPE · IPR · HBCE · MATRiX

Runtime prototype for IPR inside HBCE

JOKER-C2 is the runtime prototype used to demonstrate how IPR can operate inside the HBCE ecosystem. It connects AI-assisted interaction with operational identity, governance checks, risk classification, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof records and audit-oriented reporting.

JOKER-C2 is not the main commercial product. The base operational product is IPR. HBCE is the ecosystem where IPR works, and MATRiX is the broader architectural framework. JOKER-C2 shows the system in motion.

POSITIONING BOUNDARY

JOKER-C2 is an experimental runtime and R&D prototype. It does not replace legal review, institutional validation, regulated certification, qualified trust services, external audit certification or production-grade enterprise deployment by itself. Its function is to demonstrate identity-linked, governed and auditable AI-assisted execution.

4. IPR AI Audit Trail flow

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first concrete MVP use case demonstrated through JOKER-C2. It turns AI-assisted document analysis into a traceable workflow where IPR, document hash, AI action, validation, EVT continuity and OPC proof are connected.

1 · IPR

Attach identity

The workflow begins from an operational identity reference rather than an unbound AI interaction.

2 · DOCUMENT

Upload and hash

The document is processed through a technical hash commitment so the analyzed file can be reconstructed.

3 · AI + HUMAN

Analyze and validate

AI output remains decision-support, while human validation creates the responsibility boundary.

4 · PROOF

Generate report

EVT, OPC and an audit-ready report are generated after the governed workflow is completed.

7. What JOKER-C2 is not

JOKER-C2 must not be presented as the final product, a regulated AI certification system, a public authority infrastructure, a legal evidence authority, an eIDAS qualified trust service, an autonomous compliance engine or a production-grade enterprise SaaS platform by itself.

Its correct role is narrower and stronger: it is the runtime prototype that demonstrates how IPR, HBCE governance, EVT continuity, OPC proof and audit-ready reporting can operate together in a concrete AI-assisted workflow.

8. Pilot and deployment boundary

JOKER-C2 and IPR AI Audit Trail are currently presented as experimental R&D and pilot-ready prototypes. They do not create legal certification, regulatory approval, eIDAS qualification, public-sector adoption or external audit certification by themselves.

For research, pilot, compliance, cybersecurity governance or institutional discussion, use the official contact channels. Do not send secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, private evidence or sensitive operational payloads through public channels.
