JOKER-C2 is not accessed through a simple email account.

JOKER-C2 is the AI Operational Runtime connected to the HBCE identity, governance and proof model. It is not positioned as a generic chatbot and not as a classic AI surface where access begins with email, password and subscription.

JOKER-C2 demonstrates how AI interaction can remain connected to operational identity, context classification, policy evaluation, risk evaluation, human responsibility, EVT continuity, OPC proof records and audit-ready verification boundaries.
