Prepare an IPR record for verification.

Activate is the public preparation gateway for IPR workflows inside the HBCE ecosystem. In this context, activation means preparing an IPR operational record, generating evidence material, checking proof consistency and connecting the record to later verification surfaces.

This page does not create legal identity, public authority validation, eIDAS qualification, regulated certification, institutional authorization or production access by itself. IPR remains the base operational R&D/MVP product; HBCE is the ecosystem where IPR works; JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator; MATRiX is the architectural framework.

POSITIONING BOUNDARY

This route is a preparation and orientation gateway. It should not be interpreted as regulated activation, official issuance, legal certification, public identity registration, qualified trust-service onboarding or production authorization.

1. Preparation flow

The current R&D/MVP flow is structured as a technical preparation corridor. Each step prepares evidence for the next one. An IPR-linked record should be presented as operationally valid only after the relevant evidence can be checked under fail-closed logic.

5. Fail-closed rule

If IPR evidence cannot be reproduced through controlled material, proof references and deterministic verification, the correct state is failure or manual review. No record should be presented as valid when required evidence is missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable.
