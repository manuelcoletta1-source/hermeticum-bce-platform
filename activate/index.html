Activate IPR means review, verify and enable operational access.

Activate is the HBCE Platform gateway for moving a prepared or submitted IPR record from IPR_PENDING toward IPR_ACTIVE.

Activation is not a simple button, not automatic confirmation and not official identity issuance. It is a controlled review phase where private verification, policy checks, proof references and access-state logic determine whether an IPR can become active inside the HBCE environment.

The target sequence is: IPR_PENDING → verification review → policy check → activation EVT → OPC receipt → IPR_ACTIVE → IPR Card → governed JOKER-C2 access where allowed.

Activation flow

STEP 01

Receive IPR_PENDING

Activation begins only after an IPR record has been prepared or submitted. The correct starting state is IPR_PENDING, not IPR_ACTIVE.

STEP 02

Review private evidence

Identity evidence, document evidence, national identifier references, manual notes or liveness evidence belong to the private or controlled verification layer.

STEP 03

Check policy and risk

The pending record must be interpreted through scope, subject type, verification level, risk condition and operational policy.

STEP 04

Generate activation EVT

If activation is allowed, the state transition can be recorded as an EVT activation event for future reconstruction.

STEP 05

Issue OPC receipt

OPC can bind the activation event, IPR reference, proof state, timestamp and verification boundary into an operational proof receipt.

STEP 06

Enable IPR Card

Only after successful activation can the IPR Card represent an active access-capable operational identity.

STATE TRANSITION

Activation changes access state only after review.

Activation must be treated as a state transition. Missing, malformed, incomplete or inconsistent evidence must not produce active status.

Verification levels used during activation

L0

Technical R&D reference

Local, synthetic or technical reference used for internal tests, non-production demonstrations and hash-oriented attribution. Not suitable for verified JOKER-C2 access by itself.

L1

Basic account verification

Basic contact-level checks such as email or phone verification. This does not by itself create a document-verified operational identity.

L2

Document-based private verification

Private review of accepted identity-document evidence and controlled linkage to a national tax identifier or national identification number where applicable.

L3

Enhanced operational verification

Enhanced verification with document evidence, national identifier reference, photo, short video, liveness or manual review in a separately scoped implementation.

FAIL-CLOSED RULE

No evidence, no activation.

If IPR evidence cannot be reproduced through private verification, controlled material, proof references and deterministic verification, the correct state is failure or manual review. No record should be presented as active when required evidence is missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable. 

missing evidence

→ IPR_ACTIVE where allowed
NON-CLAIMS

Activation is operational, not official identity issuance.

  • Activation does not create legal identity.
  • Activation does not create official European identity.
  • Activation does not create public authority validation.
  • Activation does not create regulated certification.
  • Activation does not create eIDAS qualification or qualified trust-service status.
  • Activation does not create regulated KYC / AML provider status.
  • Activation does not create banking service status.
  • Activation does not authorize production use with sensitive data by itself.
  • Activation does not allow tax codes, national identifiers, identity documents or document numbers in the public registry.
  • Activation does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries or qualified trust services.

Any real production onboarding, document verification, liveness detection, biometric comparison, enterprise deployment, public-sector use or institutional integration requires separate legal, privacy, cybersecurity, compliance and operational review.
