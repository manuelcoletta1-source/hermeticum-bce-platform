Claims & Non-Claims

This page defines the scope boundaries of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. public platform. It clarifies what the current R&D/MVP system claims to demonstrate, what it does not claim, and how IPR, IPR Verified, IPR AI Audit Trail, HBCE, JOKER-C2 and MATRIX must be positioned.

The central product is IPR. IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP. HBCE is the operational ecosystem. JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator. MATRIX is the broader architectural framework.

Current posture: R&D/MVP · IPR-first · private verification · public proof minimization · EU-first · audit-first · fail-closed · no public data custody

IPR IPR Verified IPR AI Audit Trail Verify Legal

1. Product and system hierarchy

BASE PRODUCT
IPR
IPR, or Identity Primary Record, is the base operational product. It is designed to connect identity reference, event context, responsibility, proof material and continuity into a verifiable technical record.
VERIFIED PROFILE
IPR Verified
IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence in a controlled private layer, while the public layer exposes only minimized proof references.
FIRST MVP
IPR AI Audit Trail
IPR AI Audit Trail is the first demonstrable MVP workflow. It shows how AI-assisted document analysis can be connected to IPR, document hashing, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof and audit-ready reporting.
OPERATIONAL ECOSYSTEM
HBCE
HBCE is the operational ecosystem around IPR. It includes governance logic, private verification boundaries, verification surfaces, registry references, EVT continuity, OPC proof, evidence packs and fail-closed rules.
RUNTIME DEMONSTRATOR
JOKER-C2
JOKER-C2 is the runtime prototype used to demonstrate IPR in operation inside HBCE. It is not the final commercial product and should not be presented as an autonomous authority.
ARCHITECTURAL FRAMEWORK
MATRIX
MATRIX is the broader architectural framework for verifiable AI operations, institutional traceability, identity-linked execution and audit-ready digital governance.

2. Status of the claims

The claims on this page describe the intended technical and governance posture of the current public platform. They must be understood as R&D, MVP, documentation, prototype and pilot-positioning claims unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active.

These claims do not create legal certification, regulated certification, public authority approval, civil identity status, qualified trust-service status, institutional authorization, customer acceptance, production deployment authorization or legally binding evidence status by themselves.

The correct external message is: IPR is an operational identity and proof product with private verification and public proof minimization. It is not an official legal identity system, not a regulated KYC / AML provider, not an eIDAS qualified trust service and not a public authority registry.

3. What the platform claims to demonstrate

IPR
Operational identity-linked records
The platform demonstrates how an operational identity reference can be connected to events, proof material, responsibility context and verification workflows.
PRIVATE VERIFICATION
Controlled identity evidence layer
The platform defines a target model where IPR Verified may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a private verification layer, not in the public registry.
PUBLIC PROOF
Minimized proof references
The platform uses hash-oriented evidence, including payload_sha256, receipts, registry references and evidence-pack material, to support later reconstruction without public identity custody.
VERIFY
Deterministic technical verification
Verification is designed around technical checks such as public proof presence, local hash comparison, registry lookup and explicit states such as PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, INVALID and NON_OPERATIONAL.
FAIL-CLOSED
No implicit validity
Missing, malformed, inconsistent, unverifiable or ambiguous states should not produce implicit validity. They must remain invalid, non-operational or subject to manual/private review.
AI GOVERNANCE
Human-validated AI workflows
The platform frames AI as a decision-support layer that must remain connected to human validation, responsibility ownership, policy boundaries and auditability.

4. What the platform does not claim

LEGAL IDENTITY
Not a legal identity scheme
The platform does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, civil registries, national eID systems, public identity documents or qualified trust services.
KYC / AML
Not a regulated KYC / AML provider
IPR Verified may use private identity-verification evidence, but the platform does not claim regulated KYC / AML provider status, bank onboarding status, financial authorization or customer due-diligence certification.
PUBLIC ID CUSTODY
Not a public identity-document database
The public layer must not store tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, full civil identity payloads or private evidence.
CERTIFICATION
Not legal or regulated certification
A public proof match, IPR record, EVT reference, OPC proof or audit report does not automatically create legal certification, regulated certification, evidentiary status or institutional authorization.
eIDAS
Not an eIDAS qualified trust service
The platform does not claim eIDAS qualification, qualified electronic signature status, qualified timestamping status or qualified trust-service-provider status.
REGULATORY
Not regulatory approval
The platform does not claim approval by a regulator, public authority, EU institution, national government, certification body, notified body or supervisory authority.
PUBLIC SECTOR
Not public-sector adoption
Public pages, registry entries, technical documentation and pilot notes do not imply public-sector adoption, procurement award, institutional mandate, official endorsement or governmental deployment.
COMPLIANCE
Not automatic compliance
The platform does not automatically make a workflow compliant with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law, contractual obligations or sector-specific regulation.
SECURITY
Not cybersecurity certification
The platform does not certify cybersecurity assurance and does not replace secure development, SOC, incident response, endpoint protection, access control, network security, vulnerability management or production security operations.
SaaS
Not production-grade SaaS by itself
The current platform is R&D/MVP and pilot-positioned. It must not be presented as production-grade enterprise SaaS unless separate deployment, security, legal and operational requirements are satisfied.
AI AUTHORITY
Not autonomous authority
The platform does not authorize AI systems to replace human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility. AI outputs remain decision-support unless separately validated.
SURVEILLANCE
Not surveillance infrastructure
The platform does not require continuous tracking of individuals. The public layer should contain minimized proof references, not personal telemetry or behavioral monitoring.
FINANCIAL
Not a banking or financial service
The platform does not accept deposits, provide payment services, issue e-money, manage financial accounts, provide investment services, provide crypto-asset services or hold financial custody.
OPERATOR STATUS
Not operator certification
Operator references, receipts, packs or registry entries do not create certified-operator status, professional licensing, public authority approval, regulated qualification or production authorization.

5. Public proof interpretation

CAN SHOW
What public proof can demonstrate
  • a public hash-oriented proof reference exists;
  • a local payload matches an expected SHA-256 value;
  • a registry entry declares a timestamp and status field;
  • an EVT or OPC reference is present in a technical workflow;
  • an audit trail or evidence pack can support later reconstruction;
  • a public registry lookup can return PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, INVALID or NON_OPERATIONAL.
CANNOT SHOW BY ITSELF
What public proof does not prove alone
  • it does not certify civil or legal identity;
  • it does not prove that private verification was legally sufficient for a regulated purpose;
  • it does not replace private evidence review;
  • it does not create automatic legal authorization;
  • it does not replace institutional validation;
  • it does not replace regulated certification;
  • it does not validate private claims without supporting evidence;
  • it does not prove production security or regulatory compliance by itself.

6. Public data boundaries

The public layer must not contain tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, raw personal identifiers, private evidence, credentials, secrets, private keys, production logs, private communications, confidential third-party data, customer data, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive operational payloads.

Public or exportable records should be limited to minimized technical fields such as:

  • entity_type
  • subject_label
  • operator_label, where applicable
  • record_scope
  • verification_level, where applicable
  • payload_sha256
  • timestamp
  • status
  • evt_id, where applicable
  • opc_id, where applicable
  • audit_state, where applicable

Complete evidence, identity material, legal documents, credentials and sensitive payloads must remain outside the public repository and outside public registry surfaces unless there is a separate lawful, authorized and security-reviewed basis.

7. Safe use boundary

Demo and pilot workflows should use only synthetic, public or explicitly authorized materials. Users should not upload secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, tax codes, customer files, confidential third-party data, production logs or sensitive operational evidence into public demos.

Any external pilot, commercial test, institutional discussion or customer-facing deployment must define the legal basis, data boundary, security boundary, responsibility owner, validation process and evidence custody model before operational use.

Public proof supports technical reconstruction. It does not remove the need for private review, legal assessment, security assessment, contractual validation and operational authorization.

8. Why boundaries matter

In high-accountability environments, ambiguity becomes risk. Clear boundaries make adoption, audit, review and technical integration safer because each claim is limited to what the system can actually demonstrate in its current R&D/MVP state.

HBCE uses these boundaries to prevent overclaiming, reduce privacy risk, separate public proof from legal validity, and keep IPR positioned correctly as an operational identity-linked evidence product rather than a regulated identity, KYC / AML provider, qualified trust service, public authority registry or certification authority.
