Claims and non-claims for HBCE Platform.

This page defines the public scope boundaries of the HERMETICUM B.C.E. Platform. It clarifies what the current R&D / pilot system claims to demonstrate, what it does not claim, and how HBCE Platform, IPR, IPR Card, JOKER-C2, Registry, Verify, EVT, OPC and MATRIX must be positioned.

The central product is IPR. The visible operational access object is IPR Card. JOKER-C2 is the governed AI runtime demonstrator. Registry shows minimized public proof references. Verify checks public proof references. HBCE is the governance ecosystem. MATRIX is the wider architectural framework.

These claims are limited to the current public R&D, documentation, prototype and pilot-positioning posture unless a specific component is separately contracted, reviewed, validated and deployed.

CORE CLAIMS

What HBCE claims

HBCE claims:
→ IPR is an operational identity and proof record
→ IPR Card is an operational access key
→ JOKER-C2 is a governed AI runtime demonstrator
→ Registry shows minimized public proof references
→ Verify checks public proof references
→ EVT records operational events
→ OPC supports operational proof receipts
→ MATRIX is the wider architectural framework

These are technical, architectural and governance claims. They do not create official identity issuance, legal certification, regulated certification, public authority validation, eIDAS qualification, banking authorization, KYC/AML provider status, production compliance or automatic access rights by themselves.

CORE NON-CLAIMS

What HBCE does not claim

HBCE does not claim:
→ official identity issuance
→ official European identity issuance
→ eIDAS qualification
→ qualified trust-service status
→ regulated KYC / AML provider status
→ banking or financial authorization
→ public authority validation
→ automatic JOKER-C2 access
→ legal certification
→ public-sector adoption
→ cybersecurity certification
→ production-grade SaaS readiness
→ automatic compliance

The platform must not be presented as a bank, public identity provider, SPID replacement, CIE replacement, EUDI Wallet replacement, eIDAS qualified trust service, regulated KYC provider, AML provider, public authority registry or certification authority.

Product and system hierarchy

PLATFORM

HBCE Platform

HBCE Platform is the IPR Onboarding Gateway. It is designed to register, structure, verify and prepare operational identity references before governed AI access. It is not a public authority identity system.

PRODUCT

IPR

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is the base operational identity and proof record. It connects subject, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility, continuity and access state.

ACCESS OBJECT

IPR Card

IPR Card is the operational access key derived from an IPR record. It is not a state identity document, not CIE, not SPID, not EUDI Wallet, not a qualified eIDAS certificate and not a banking card.

RUNTIME

JOKER-C2

JOKER-C2 is the AI Operational Runtime and R&D demonstrator. It is not a generic chatbot, not an autonomous authority and not an AI certification body.

PUBLIC PROOF

Registry and Verify

Registry exposes minimized public proof references. Verify checks public proof references. Neither one certifies official identity or grants runtime access by itself.

FRAMEWORK

MATRIX

MATRIX is the broader architectural framework for verifiable operations, governance and institutional traceability. It is not the first product and not a regulated infrastructure status by itself.

STATUS OF CLAIMS

R&D, MVP and pilot-positioning status

The claims on this page describe the intended technical and governance posture of the current public platform. They must be understood as R&D, MVP, documentation, prototype and pilot-positioning claims unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active.

These claims do not create legal certification, regulated certification, public authority approval, civil identity status, qualified trust-service status, institutional authorization, customer acceptance, production deployment authorization or legally binding evidence status by themselves.

The correct external message is: IPR is an operational identity and proof record with private verification and public proof minimization. It is not official legal identity, not a regulated KYC / AML provider, not an eIDAS qualified trust service and not a public authority registry.

What the platform claims to demonstrate

IPR

Operational identity-linked records

The platform demonstrates how an operational identity reference can be connected to role, operation, event, proof material, responsibility context and verification workflow.

IPR CARD

Operational access key

The platform demonstrates how an active or pending IPR state can be represented through an operational card model connected to verification level, EVT, OPC and public hash reference.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION

Controlled identity evidence layer

The platform defines a target model where identity evidence belongs to a private verification layer, while the public layer exposes only minimized proof references.

PUBLIC PROOF

Minimized proof references

The platform uses hash-oriented evidence, including payload_sha256, EVT references, OPC receipts, registry entries and evidence-pack material.

VERIFY

Deterministic technical verification

Verification is designed around public proof presence, local hash comparison, registry lookup and explicit states such as PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, INVALID and NON_OPERATIONAL.

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

Missing, malformed, inconsistent, unverifiable or ambiguous states should not produce implicit validity. They must remain invalid, non-operational or subject to manual/private review.

JOKER-C2

Governed AI runtime model

The platform demonstrates an AI runtime model where access and operation are connected to verified IPR identity, policy, risk, EVT, OPC and audit continuity.

AI GOVERNANCE

Human-validated AI workflows

The platform frames AI as a decision-support layer connected to human validation, responsibility ownership, policy boundaries and auditability.

AUDITABILITY

Reviewable technical process

The platform demonstrates technical structures for later reconstruction: records, events, receipts, hashes, registry entries, verification states and proof packages.

What the platform does not claim

LEGAL IDENTITY

Not a legal identity scheme

The platform does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, civil registries, national eID systems, public identity documents or qualified trust services.

IPR CARD

Not an official identity card

IPR Card is an operational HBCE card. It is not an identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet, qualified eIDAS certificate, banking card or regulated payment instrument.

KYC / AML

Not a regulated KYC / AML provider

IPR Verified may use private identity-verification evidence, but the platform does not claim regulated KYC / AML provider status, bank onboarding status, financial authorization or customer due-diligence certification.

PUBLIC ID CUSTODY

Not a public identity-document database

The public layer must not store tax codes, document numbers, national identifiers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, liveness videos, civil identity payloads or private evidence.

CERTIFICATION

Not legal or regulated certification

A public proof match, IPR record, IPR Card, EVT reference, OPC proof or audit report does not automatically create legal certification, regulated certification, evidentiary status or institutional authorization.

eIDAS

Not an eIDAS qualified trust service

The platform does not claim eIDAS qualification, qualified electronic signature status, qualified timestamping status or qualified trust-service-provider status.

REGULATORY

Not regulatory approval

The platform does not claim approval by a regulator, public authority, EU institution, national government, certification body, notified body or supervisory authority.

PUBLIC SECTOR

Not public-sector adoption

Public pages, registry entries, technical documentation and pilot notes do not imply public-sector adoption, procurement award, institutional mandate, official endorsement or governmental deployment.

COMPLIANCE

Not automatic compliance

The platform does not automatically make a workflow compliant with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law, contractual obligations or sector-specific regulation.

SECURITY

Not cybersecurity certification

The platform does not certify cybersecurity assurance and does not replace secure development, SOC, incident response, endpoint protection, access control, network security, vulnerability management or production security operations.

FINANCIAL

Not a banking or financial service

The platform does not accept deposits, provide payment services, issue e-money, manage financial accounts, provide investment services, provide crypto-asset services or hold financial custody.

JOKER-C2 ACCESS

Not automatic runtime access

A registry entry, IPR reference, IPR Card reference or public proof match does not automatically grant JOKER-C2 access. Access depends on active IPR state, policy, risk and operational authorization.

Public proof interpretation

CAN SHOW

What public proof can demonstrate

  • A public hash-oriented proof reference exists.
  • A local payload matches an expected SHA-256 value.
  • A registry entry declares a timestamp and public status field.
  • An IPR, IPR Card, EVT or OPC reference is present in a technical workflow.
  • An audit trail or evidence pack can support later reconstruction.
  • A public registry lookup can return PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, INVALID or NON_OPERATIONAL.
CANNOT SHOW BY ITSELF

What public proof does not prove alone

  • It does not certify civil or legal identity.
  • It does not prove that private verification was legally sufficient for a regulated purpose.
  • It does not replace private evidence review.
  • It does not create automatic legal authorization.
  • It does not replace institutional validation.
  • It does not replace regulated certification.
  • It does not validate private claims without supporting evidence.
  • It does not prove production security or regulatory compliance by itself.
  • It does not authorize JOKER-C2 access by itself.
PUBLIC DATA BOUNDARIES

Public layer must remain minimized.

The public layer must not contain tax codes, fiscal codes, national identifiers, document numbers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, liveness videos, raw personal identifiers, private evidence, credentials, secrets, private keys, production logs, private communications, confidential third-party data, customer data, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive operational payloads.

Public or exportable records should be limited to minimized technical fields such as: 

ipr_id / public IPR reference
ipr_card_id / public IPR Card reference
subject_label
operator_label
record_scope
verification_level
public status
payload_sha256
timestamp
activation EVT reference
OPC receipt reference
audit_state where applicable

Complete evidence, identity material, legal documents, credentials and sensitive payloads must remain outside the public repository and outside public registry surfaces unless there is a separate lawful, authorized and security-reviewed basis.

SAFE USE BOUNDARY

Use only authorized, public, synthetic or non-sensitive material in demos.

Demo and pilot workflows should use only synthetic, public or explicitly authorized materials. Users should not upload secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, tax codes, national identifiers, customer files, confidential third-party data, production logs or sensitive operational evidence into public demos.

Any external pilot, commercial test, institutional discussion or customer-facing deployment must define the legal basis, data boundary, security boundary, responsibility owner, validation process and evidence custody model before operational use.

Public proof supports technical reconstruction. It does not remove the need for private review, legal assessment, security assessment, contractual validation and operational authorization.

WHY BOUNDARIES MATTER

Clear boundaries make the platform safer to evaluate.

In high-accountability environments, ambiguity becomes risk. Clear boundaries make adoption, audit, review and technical integration safer because each claim is limited to what the system can actually demonstrate in its current R&D / pilot state.

HBCE uses these boundaries to prevent overclaiming, reduce privacy risk, separate public proof from legal validity, and keep IPR positioned correctly as an operational identity-linked evidence product rather than a regulated identity provider, KYC / AML provider, qualified trust service, public authority registry, bank, financial institution or certification authority.
