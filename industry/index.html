3. IPR Verified boundary for industrial pilots

IPR Verified may be relevant for industrial pilots where a stronger operational identity posture is required. It uses private verification based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence, while the public layer remains minimized.

Accepted private verification document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license. This evidence must remain in the private verification layer and must not be published in the public registry, public repository, public demo pages or public issues. 

PRIVATE VERIFICATION
→ IPR VERIFIED
→ HASH-ORIENTED PUBLIC PROOF REFERENCE

IPR Verified does not create legal identity, regulated KYC / AML compliance, eIDAS qualification, public authority approval or production authorization by itself.

7. Recommended pilot path

A responsible industrial pilot should start with a non-sensitive document workflow.

  • duration: 30 days;
  • users: 1–3 authorized operators;
  • IPR profile: IPR Base, IPR Verified or IPR Professional depending on scope;
  • documents: 10–20 public, synthetic or explicitly authorized documents;
  • workflow: one AI-assisted document review process;
  • output: audit-ready reports;
  • review: traceability, human validation, report clarity and risk classification.

Any pilot involving confidential, regulated, personal, cybersecurity-sensitive or operational data requires prior legal, security and privacy review.
