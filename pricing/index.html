IPR pilot packaging and R&D catalogue

This page describes an indicative R&D/MVP catalogue for the IPR-first HBCE Platform. IPR is the base operational product. IPR Base is the technical R&D profile. IPR Verified is the private-verification profile based on tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. IPR Professional is the future business / audit workflow profile built on IPR Verified.

The first practical commercial path is IPR AI Audit Trail: a controlled AI-assisted document workflow with IPR attribution, document hash, human validation, EVT / OPC references and audit-ready reporting.

Prices and packages on this page are indicative commercial hypotheses for scoping, pilot discussion and partner review. They are not a binding public offer, not regulated certification, not official identity issuance, not eIDAS qualification, not regulated KYC / AML service pricing and not production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness by themselves.

1. How to read this page

This catalogue should be read as a pilot-packaging and R&D service map. It is designed to help define what can be tested, demonstrated, reviewed and scoped with a partner. It does not imply that every listed tier is already a deployed commercial service.

IPR BASE

Technical R&D record

IPR Base is useful for local testing, internal proof flows, hash-oriented attribution and controlled demonstrations. It does not create verified identity status.

IPR VERIFIED

Private verification profile

IPR Verified is prepared through a private identity-verification workflow using tax-code and valid identity-document evidence. The public layer stores only minimized proof references.

IPR PROFESSIONAL

Business / audit profile

IPR Professional is the future professional profile for B2B pilots, AI governance workflows, audit-ready evidence chains and controlled business use.

Any real customer pilot, institutional test, enterprise deployment or regulated-sector use requires separate scoping, legal review, cybersecurity review, privacy review, contractual definition and operational validation.

7. Private verification and public data boundary

IPR Verified may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private verification layer. Accepted document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license.

The public layer must never store tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, identity scans, portrait images, personal payloads, customer personal data, credentials, secrets, private keys, confidential third-party data, production logs, private prompts, private outputs or regulated evidence requiring protected handling.

Any real-data deployment requires a separate environment with legal basis, data-processing terms, retention rules, access controls, security controls, responsibility owners and contractual scope.
