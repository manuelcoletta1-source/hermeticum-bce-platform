IPR pilot packaging and R&D service catalogue

This page describes an indicative R&D/MVP catalogue for the IPR-first HBCE platform. IPR — Identity Primary Record is the base operational product. IPR AI Audit Trail is the first pilot use case. HBCE is the operational ecosystem. JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator. MATRiX is the architectural framework.

Prices and packages on this page are indicative commercial hypotheses for scoping, pilot discussion and partner review. They are not a binding public offer, not regulated certification, not official identity issuance, not eIDAS qualification and not production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness by themselves.

1. How to read this page

This catalogue should be read as a pilot-packaging and R&D service map. It is designed to help define what can be tested, demonstrated, reviewed and scoped with a partner. It does not imply that every listed tier is already a deployed commercial service.

PRODUCT

IPR

IPR is the base operational product. It connects an operational identity reference, event context, responsibility layer, proof material and verification path.

PILOT USE CASE

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. It converts AI-assisted document work into an IPR-linked, human-validated and audit-ready technical sequence.

Any real customer pilot, institutional test, enterprise deployment or regulated-sector use requires separate scoping, legal review, cybersecurity review, privacy review, contractual definition and operational validation.

2. Recommended first commercial path

The recommended initial commercial path is not a large infrastructure sale. The first practical path is a controlled IPR AI Audit Trail pilot for organizations or advisors that need to make AI-assisted document workflows more reconstructable and auditable.

6. Data and security boundary

Public demos and R&D pilots must use only synthetic, public, owned or expressly authorized materials. Do not upload or process secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, customer personal data, confidential third-party data, production logs, private prompts, private outputs or regulated evidence requiring protected handling.

Any real-data deployment requires a separate environment with legal basis, data-processing terms, retention rules, access controls, security controls, responsibility owners and contractual scope.

7. What this catalogue does not claim

  • It does not create legal certification.
  • It does not create legal identity or official identity issuance.
  • It does not create eIDAS qualification or qualified trust-service status.
  • It does not create public authority approval or institutional adoption.
  • It does not create automated compliance with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law or cybersecurity law.
  • It does not create production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness by itself.
  • It does not replace legal, privacy, cybersecurity, procurement, compliance or external audit review.
  • It does not constitute a binding offer, price guarantee or procurement proposal without a separate written agreement.
