This page describes the adoption tiers of the HBCE system. Pricing is not presented as generic software subscription logic. It is structured around identity entry, governance continuity, registry discipline, verification readiness, and infrastructure posture.

How to read this page

Each tier combines two dimensions: entry posture and continuity posture. Entry posture concerns initial identity, structure, and system alignment. Continuity posture concerns governance, verification, registry discipline, and long-term operational proof.
