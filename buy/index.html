Request options

IPR BASE

Technical R&D record

IPR Base is used for technical tests, proof flows, internal demonstrations and controlled R&D workflows. It does not create verified identity status.

Indicative scope only · no verified identity claim · no certification claim.

IPR VERIFIED

Private verification profile

IPR Verified may use accepted identity evidence and national identifier evidence only in a controlled private layer. Public proof remains minimized and non-custodial.

Private verification boundary required · no public document custody · not KYC / AML provider onboarding.

IPR CARD

Operational access key

IPR Card is the operational card derived from an IPR record. It may represent verification level, access state, activation EVT, OPC receipt and public proof reference.

Not identity card · not SPID · not EUDI Wallet · not eIDAS qualified certificate.

FIRST MVP

IPR AI Audit Trail

A controlled AI-assisted document workflow with IPR reference, document hash, governance decision, human validation, EVT, OPC and audit-ready reporting.

First MVP · external pilots require scoping · authorized non-sensitive documents only.

JOKER-C2

Governed runtime discussion

JOKER-C2 access is not sold as generic AI access. It may be discussed only as governed runtime access connected to IPR state, policy, risk and pilot scope.

No automatic runtime access · no generic subscription claim.

B2B / B2G

Enterprise or institutional review

Pilot discussion for compliance, cybersecurity, legal operations, internal audit, public-sector innovation and AI governance teams.

No public-sector adoption, procurement award or institutional approval implied.
