1. Request boundary

This page supports preliminary business, R&D and pilot-package requests. It does not create an automatic contract, license, regulated certification, public authority approval, procurement status or production deployment.

Any real pilot, commercial agreement, enterprise use, institutional use or private identity-verification workflow requires a separate written scope, legal review, privacy review, cybersecurity review, contractual definition and operational validation.

Do not send tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, private evidence, credentials, secrets, customer files, production logs or confidential third-party material in the first request.

2. Package options

IPR BASE

Technical R&D record

IPR Base is used for technical tests, proof flows, internal demonstrations and controlled R&D workflows. It does not create verified identity status.

Indicative scope only · no certification claim

IPR VERIFIED

Private verification profile

IPR Verified may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private layer. Public proof remains minimized and non-custodial.

Private verification boundary required · no public document custody

IPR PROFESSIONAL

Business / audit profile

IPR Professional is a future business / audit workflow profile based on IPR Verified, intended for controlled B2B pilots and audit-ready evidence chains.

Pilot scope required · no production SaaS claim

MVP

IPR AI Audit Trail

A controlled AI-assisted document workflow with IPR reference, document hash, governance decision, human validation, EVT, OPC and audit-ready reporting.

First MVP use case · R&D pilot-ready

B2B

Enterprise pilot review

Pilot discussion for compliance, cybersecurity, legal operations, internal audit and AI governance teams. Scope depends on data boundary and review requirements.

Requires legal, privacy and security scoping

B2G

Institutional R&D review

Public-sector-relevant R&D discussion for non-sensitive documentation, AI governance and auditability workflows.

No public-sector adoption or procurement claim
