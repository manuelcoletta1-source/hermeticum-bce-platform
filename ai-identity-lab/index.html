AI Identity & Compliance Lab

HBCE R&D develops identity and governance layers for AI systems operating in regulated environments.

Problem

Most AI systems lack formal operational identity and structured accountability. This limits deployment in enterprise and regulated contexts.

  • No agent-level identity binding
  • No deterministic authorization boundary
  • No append-only decision evidence
  • No fail-closed architecture

Approach

Input ↓ Identity Gate ↓ Policy Boundary ↓ AI Execution ↓ Append-Only Evidence ↓ Output

The layer does not replace AI models. It introduces structural control and traceability.

Pilot Program

HBCE R&D collaborates with selected AI software companies in Torino / Piemonte.

  • Architecture review
  • Identity boundary definition
  • Prototype integration
  • Technical compliance report

Duration: 4–6 weeks.
