AI Identity & Compliance Lab

Operational identity and governance layer for AI systems under EU regulatory context. Developed by HBCE R&D — Torino.

The Problem

Most AI systems operate without a formal identity boundary. They generate output, but lack verifiable operational accountability.

  • No identity binding to AI agent
  • No deterministic authorization layer
  • No structured append-only decision evidence
  • No fail-closed architecture
  • Audit posture remains technical, not structural

The HBCE Approach

HBCE R&D develops an Identity & Governance Layer that integrates between AI execution and system output. 

Input
↓
Identity Gate
↓
Policy Boundary
↓
AI Execution
↓
Append-Only Evidence
↓
Output

The layer does not replace AI models. It makes them governable and verifiable.

Use Cases

  • Enterprise chatbots
  • Decision-support AI
  • AI-driven workflow automation
  • SaaS platforms with embedded AI
  • Autonomous decision systems

Pilot Program — Torino / Piemonte

HBCE R&D selects up to 3 AI software companies for technical pilot integration.

  • 4–6 week collaboration
  • Architecture review
  • Identity boundary definition
  • Integration prototype
  • Compliance-oriented technical report

Objective: make AI systems audit-ready and structurally governable.
