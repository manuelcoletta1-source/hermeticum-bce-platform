European Cybernetic Bank — R&D concept

“European Cybernetic Bank” is a research metaphor for an operational data-clearing layer: a system that studies how IPR references, public proof commitments, event records, verification and audit trails can be structured without public custody of private evidence.

The current concrete MVP connected to this concept is IPR AI Audit Trail. MATRIX remains the wider architectural framework.

It is not a bank, not a credit institution, not a payment institution, not an e-money institution, not a financial intermediary, not a regulated financial service, not a crypto-asset service provider and not an institution of the European Union.

2. Why “bank” is used as a metaphor

The term “bank” is used in the sense of structured custody discipline, clearing discipline, audit discipline and verification discipline applied to operational data references.

In the current HBCE model, the public layer should not store private evidence. Instead, it should expose minimized proof commitments and allow deterministic verification.

Clearing
Proof matching
Registry v3 and payload_sha256 as public proof reference.
Audit
Reviewability
Append-only records, audit findings and event-style continuity.
Minimization
No custody
Private evidence remains outside the public repository and registry.
Fail-closed
No soft validity
Missing or malformed proof must not produce validity.

4. What the public layer may contain

The public layer may contain minimized non-sensitive proof references such as:

  • payload_sha256 commitments;
  • timestamps;
  • public labels;
  • record scope;
  • status metadata;
  • audit findings;
  • non-sensitive public documentation.

The public layer must not contain private identity evidence, identity documents, credentials, private keys, personal data beyond minimized public labels, customer data, production logs, private communications or sensitive operational payloads.

6. Policy interpretation

GDPR-min: public proof should remain hash-only or metadata-minimized.
Fail-closed: if the registry source is unavailable or malformed, verification must not return validity.
Append-only: new evidence should be added as new records, not silently rewritten.
Client-side verification: browser-side checks may support public proof review but do not replace private validation.
Policy: EU_FIRST · AUDIT_FIRST · FAIL_CLOSED · HASH_ONLY · APPEND_ONLY · GDPR_MIN · NO_PUBLIC_DATA_CUSTODY · NO_FINANCIAL_CUSTODY
