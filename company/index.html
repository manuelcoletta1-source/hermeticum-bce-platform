HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. is the operating and R&D vehicle behind HBCE Platform. It develops operational identity, proof, audit and governed AI access models.

The current product focus is IPR — Identity Primary Record. The visible operational access object is IPR Card. The first MVP is IPR AI Audit Trail. The governed runtime demonstrator is JOKER-C2. HBCE is the governance ecosystem and MATRIX is the wider architectural framework.

The company is the pilot, partner, enterprise and institutional contact point for scoped R&D, controlled validation and operational review. It does not claim public authority status, banking authorization, official identity issuance, regulated KYC / AML provider status, eIDAS qualification or qualified trust-service status.

IPR CARD ISSUANCE BOUNDARY

Operational issuer inside HBCE, not public identity authority.

HERMETICUM B.C.E. S.r.l. may issue or model IPR Card as a digital operational card inside the HBCE environment. This does not replace identity card, passport, CIE, SPID, EUDI Wallet or qualified eIDAS certificate unless future integrations with recognized identity or trust-service systems are separately established.

HBCE does not issue official European identity. HBCE issues a verifiable operational identity reference that may be connected to official European identity systems in future compliant integrations.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION BOUNDARY

Private verification stays private.

IPR Verified strengthens the professional positioning of the platform through private identity verification. It may use accepted identity evidence and national identifier evidence only in a controlled private verification layer.

The public layer must never store tax codes, national identifiers, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portrait images, liveness videos, biometric material, private evidence, personal data or customer payloads.
