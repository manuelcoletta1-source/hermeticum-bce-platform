3. Platform focus

IPR

IPR is the base operational product. It connects subject, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility and continuity.

IPR Verified

IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private layer.

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. It turns AI-assisted document work into an IPR-linked audit-ready sequence.

HBCE

HBCE is the operational ecosystem around IPR, including governance, registry, verification, EVT, OPC and fail-closed rules.

JOKER-C2

JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator used to show IPR operating inside governed AI-assisted workflows.

MATRiX

MATRiX is the broader architectural framework. It is not the first product to sell; the first product is IPR.

4. IPR Verified private verification boundary

IPR Verified strengthens the professional positioning of the platform through private identity verification. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private verification layer.

Accepted document categories are identity card / CIE, passport and driving license.

The public layer must never store tax codes, fiscal codes, document numbers, identity documents, scans, portrait images, biometric material, private evidence, personal data or customer payloads.
