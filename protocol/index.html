Protocol defines how HBCE records are structured, checked and interpreted.

The Protocol section contains R&D technical references for IPR-linked evidence, IPR Card references, public proof records, EVT events, OPC receipts, registry semantics, verification states and fail-closed interpretation.

Protocol does not create legal validity by itself. These files are not a formal legal standard, not regulated technical certification, not public authority approval, not eIDAS qualification, not regulated KYC / AML status and not a production compliance framework by themselves.

The current implementation direction is IPR-first: IPR is the operational product, IPR Card is the operational access key, JOKER-C2 is the governed AI runtime demonstrator, Registry / Verify provide public proof and deterministic checks, and IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP.
