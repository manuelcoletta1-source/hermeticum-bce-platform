HBCE Operational Architecture

HBCE is a layered infrastructure for operational identity and certified events across humans, AI, machines and critical systems.

Core operational layers

IPR — Identity Layer
Persistent operational identity for humans, AI, robots, vehicles and infrastructures.
Registry — Public Proof
Append-only public registry with hash-only verification and timestamp integrity.
IO-SPACE — Certified Events
Transforms actions into verifiable operational events.
CyberGlobal — Integrity Defense
Protection of identities, access and operational infrastructure.
NeuroLoop — Predictive Layer
Analysis and prediction based on verified operational data.
JOKER-C2 — Command Layer
Operational command and coordination across the network.

Operational principles

Audit-first
Every critical action produces verifiable proof.
Fail-closed
If identity or event is not valid, system blocks.
Hash-only public
No public storage of personal data.
