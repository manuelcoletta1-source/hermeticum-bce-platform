The architecture is not email-to-AI.

The HBCE Platform architecture is verified operational identity to governed AI runtime. It does not start from a generic account, email and subscription. It starts from IPR onboarding, operational identity, verification state, IPR Card, proof continuity, policy gates, risk gates and fail-closed access.

The stack connects HBCE Platform, IPR Onboarding Gateway, IPR record, IPR Card, EVT, OPC, Registry, Verify, JOKER-C2, IPR AI Audit Trail and the Legal / Privacy / Claims boundary layer.

The architecture is currently an R&D / pilot architecture. It is not official identity issuance, not eIDAS qualification, not regulated KYC / AML provider status, not public authority validation, not banking service and not production-grade enterprise SaaS readiness by itself.

The architecture is stateful. A draft, pending, active, suspended, revoked or expired IPR cannot be treated the same way. Access and operation depend on state, proof, policy and review.

IPR state and access logic

NO_IPR
→ ACCESS_DENIED

IPR_DRAFT
→ ACCESS_DENIED

IPR_PENDING
→ REVIEW_REQUIRED

IPR_REVIEW_REQUIRED
→ HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED

IPR_ACTIVE + policy pass + acceptable risk + required proof
→ access may be allowed

IPR_SUSPENDED
→ ACCESS_DENIED

IPR_REVOKED
→ ACCESS_DENIED

IPR_EXPIRED
→ RENEWAL_REQUIRED

IPR_ACTIVE is an access condition, not an unconditional right to operate. HBCE governance remains active across context, risk, policy, proof and human review.

Operational principles

AUDIT-FIRST

Reviewable continuity

Critical workflows should produce verifiable references, state transitions and audit-ready reconstruction.

FAIL-CLOSED

No implicit validity

If identity, proof, registry, policy, risk or verification fails, the operation cannot be treated as valid.

HASH-ORIENTED PUBLIC LAYER

Minimized exposure

Public records should contain minimized proof references, not private identity evidence or sensitive payloads.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

AI is support

AI outputs remain decision-support unless separately reviewed, validated and accepted by responsible humans or organizations.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION

Controlled identity evidence

Verification evidence belongs to private or controlled environments, not public registry or public repository content.

BOUNDARY LAYER

Legal / Privacy / Claims

Boundary pages define non-claims, privacy limits, terms, security posture and compliance posture.

NON-CLAIMS

What this architecture does not claim

  • It is not official identity issuance.
  • It is not a public authority infrastructure.
  • It is not a civil identity registry.
  • It is not a legal certification system.
  • It is not eIDAS qualification or qualified trust-service status.
  • It is not regulated KYC / AML provider status.
  • It is not banking, payment or financial authorization.
  • It is not public-sector adoption or procurement award.
  • It is not automatic compliance with GDPR, AI Act, NIS2, eIDAS, procurement law, cybersecurity law or sector-specific regulation.
  • It does not replace external audit, legal review, cybersecurity review, institutional validation or production governance.
