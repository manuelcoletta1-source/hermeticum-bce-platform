Governance decides whether an operational identity can act.

HBCE Platform defines a governance model for IPR onboarding, IPR Card status, public proof references, access decisions, responsible AI workflows and fail-closed verification.

JOKER-C2 does not bypass HBCE governance. A simple email account is not sufficient. An active IPR state is not always sufficient by itself. Access and operation depend on IPR state, verification level, policy, risk, context, proof references and human or organizational review where required.

The governance objective is to keep system states reviewable, attributable, reconstructible and resistant to ambiguous validity, while preserving human responsibility, privacy minimization and clear non-claims.

GOVERNANCE STATUS

Unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active, the material should be treated as experimental, prototypical, conceptual, documentary or pilot-positioning material.

CANONICAL GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Governance principles

  • EU-first: EU regulation is the default governance reference point, without implying certification, endorsement or adoption.
  • Audit-first: relevant records, changes, validations and decisions should remain reviewable and reconstructible.
  • Private verification: identity evidence belongs only in a controlled private layer.
  • Public proof minimization: the public layer exposes minimized proof references, not personal identity payloads.
  • Fail-closed: missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable states must not produce implicit validity.
  • IPR-linked evidence: operational records should connect identity reference, event context, responsibility, proof and continuity.
  • Policy gate: an operation must be evaluated against declared scope and allowed-use boundaries.
  • Risk gate: higher-risk operations require review, restriction, escalation or denial.
  • Human responsibility: AI-assisted workflows must remain connected to human validation and responsibility ownership.
  • No public data custody: public routes must not store tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, credentials, secrets or sensitive payloads.

Governance decision logic

IDENTITY GATE

Who is acting?

The system checks whether a subject has an IPR reference, IPR Card state, verification level and allowed operational role.

POLICY GATE

Is the action allowed?

The system evaluates whether the requested action fits the declared scope, allowed-use boundary and public policy constraints.

RISK GATE

Does it require escalation?

Higher-risk operations can require human review, restricted output, denial, logging, EVT generation or explicit proof capture.

PROOF GATE

Can it be reconstructed?

The system checks whether the operation can be connected to hash references, EVT, OPC, registry entries or evidence-pack material.

HUMAN REVIEW

Who validates?

Human or organizational review is required when policy, risk, evidence or responsibility conditions are not automatically satisfied.

ACCESS DECISION

Allow, deny or review?

The runtime decision can be access allowed, access denied, review required, renewal required, suspension or revocation.

PRIVATE VERIFICATION GOVERNANCE

Private identity evidence stays outside public governance surfaces.

IPR Verified may use private identity-verification evidence. This evidence must remain outside the public layer. The public registry may expose only minimized proof references, not the underlying identity material.

Public proof interpretation

CAN SHOW

What public proof can show

  • A public proof reference is present.
  • A hash matches an expected commitment.
  • A timestamped public record exists.
  • A public status field has been declared.
  • An EVT or OPC reference may support technical reconstruction.
  • An audit trace records a review or remediation step.
CANNOT SHOW BY ITSELF

What public proof does not show by itself

  • It does not certify civil or legal identity.
  • It does not prove regulated KYC / AML compliance.
  • It does not replace private evidence review.
  • It does not create automatic legal authorization.
  • It does not replace institutional validation.
  • It does not replace regulated certification.
  • It does not create automatic compliance.
  • It does not validate private claims without supporting evidence.
AI GOVERNANCE AND HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

AI is support. Responsibility remains human or organizational.

Within the IPR / HBCE / JOKER-C2 / MATRIX research line, AI is treated as a support layer, not as an autonomous replacement for human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility.

  • Human validation;
  • Responsibility ownership;
  • Policy constraints;
  • Risk classification;
  • Audit metadata;
  • Event continuity;
  • Private/public data separation;
  • Fail-closed escalation when required conditions are missing.

If an AI-assisted workflow cannot preserve accountability, validation or auditability, it should block, escalate, restrict or require human review.
