Governance

HBCE Platform defines a governance model for IPR-linked operational records, private verification boundaries, public proof references, audit-ready reconstruction, responsible AI workflows and fail-closed verification. The current platform is centered on IPR as the operational R&D/MVP product, IPR Verified as the private-verification profile, IPR AI Audit Trail as the first demonstrable use case, HBCE as the operational ecosystem, JOKER-C2 as the runtime demonstrator and MATRiX as the architectural framework.

The governance objective is to make system states reviewable, attributable, reconstructible and resistant to ambiguous validity, while preserving human responsibility, public proof minimization and clear non-claims.

Policy: EU-first · audit-first · private verification · IPR-linked evidence · hash-oriented public proof · fail-closed · no public data custody

1. Governance status

HBCE Platform is an experimental R&D public gateway. Its governance model is designed for research, documentation, auditability, private verification boundaries, public proof-reference verification, operational identity research and responsible AI governance.

Unless a specific component is separately documented as implemented, validated, contracted and operationally active, the material should be treated as experimental, prototypical, conceptual, documentary or pilot-positioning material.

Governance language in this repository does not create automatic legal certification, public authority approval, regulated qualification, civil identity status, institutional authorization, external audit certification, automated compliance, regulated KYC / AML provider status or production-grade enterprise readiness.

2. System hierarchy

IPR

IPR — Identity Primary Record — is the base operational product. It links an operational identity reference to event context, responsibility, proof material, continuity and verification. It is not a legal identity scheme and does not replace SPID, CIE, EUDI Wallet, eIDAS schemes, public registries, digital signatures or qualified trust services.

IPR Verified

IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private layer. Public proof must remain minimized and must not expose tax codes, document numbers, identity documents or personal payloads.

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail is the first MVP use case. It demonstrates how an AI-assisted document workflow can be connected to IPR, document hashing, governance decisioning, human validation, EVT continuity, OPC proof and audit-ready reporting.

HBCE, JOKER-C2 and MATRiX

HBCE is the operational ecosystem containing governance logic, private verification boundaries, registry references, verification surfaces, evidence packs, EVT continuity, OPC proof and fail-closed rules. JOKER-C2 is the runtime demonstrator used to show IPR in operation inside governed AI-assisted workflows. MATRiX is the broader architectural framework.

3. Canonical governance principles

  • EU-first: the European regulatory environment is used as the default governance reference point, without implying certification, endorsement or adoption by any public authority.
  • Audit-first: relevant records, changes, validations and decisions should remain reviewable and reconstructible.
  • Private verification: IPR Verified may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only inside a controlled private layer.
  • Public proof minimization: the public layer should expose only minimized proof references, not personal identity payloads.
  • Fail-closed: missing, malformed, inconsistent or unverifiable states must not produce implicit validity.
  • IPR-linked evidence: operational records should connect identity reference, event context, responsibility, proof and continuity.
  • Hash-oriented public proof: the public layer should use minimized proof references, primarily payload_sha256 or equivalent digest commitments.
  • Append-only discipline: public evidence history should be extended through new records, events or findings, not silently rewritten.
  • No public data custody: the public repository and registry must not store tax codes, document numbers, identity documents, credentials, secrets, private keys, customer files or sensitive operational payloads.
  • Human responsibility: AI-assisted workflows must remain connected to human validation, responsibility ownership, auditability and policy constraints.

4. Operational rules

Create

Creation surfaces should prepare IPR-linked local or controlled artifacts, minimized public proof references and audit-ready metadata. IPR Base remains a technical R&D record, while IPR Verified requires a separate private verification boundary.

Open Create

Registry

The registry is a public proof-reference layer. It should contain minimized records based on fields such as entity_type, subject_label, verification_level, payload_sha256, timestamp and status.

Open Registry

Verify

Verification should return explicit states such as PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PRESENT, NO_PUBLIC_RECORD, INVALID or NON_OPERATIONAL.

Open Verify

Governance rule: if a required proof, registry source, schema, private verification state, human validation, responsibility owner or audit record is missing or inconsistent, the workflow should block, deny, escalate or require manual review.

5. IPR Verified governance boundary

IPR Verified may use private identity-verification evidence. This evidence must remain outside the public layer. The public registry may expose only a minimized proof reference, not the underlying identity material.

Private verification layer

  • tax code or fiscal code;
  • identity card / CIE;
  • passport;
  • driving license;
  • private evidence package;
  • controlled verification status.

Public proof layer

  • subject_label;
  • verification_level;
  • payload_sha256;
  • timestamp;
  • status;
  • EVT / OPC reference where applicable.

Public proof supports reconstruction. It does not create legal identity, regulated KYC / AML provider status, eIDAS qualification, public authority validation or institutional approval by itself.

6. Public proof interpretation

What public proof can show

  • a public proof reference is present;
  • a hash matches an expected commitment;
  • a timestamped public record exists;
  • a public status field has been declared;
  • an EVT or OPC reference may support technical reconstruction;
  • an audit trace records a review or remediation step.

What public proof does not show by itself

  • it does not certify civil or legal identity;
  • it does not prove regulated KYC / AML compliance;
  • it does not replace private evidence review;
  • it does not create automatic legal authorization;
  • it does not replace institutional validation;
  • it does not replace regulated certification;
  • it does not create automatic compliance;
  • it does not validate private claims without supporting evidence.

7. AI governance and human responsibility

Within the IPR / HBCE / JOKER-C2 / MATRiX research line, AI is treated as a support layer, not as an autonomous replacement for human, legal, contractual, institutional or professional responsibility.

Responsible AI workflows should preserve:

  • human validation;
  • responsibility ownership;
  • policy constraints;
  • risk classification;
  • audit metadata;
  • event continuity;
  • private/public data separation;
  • fail-closed escalation when required conditions are missing.

If an AI-assisted workflow cannot preserve accountability, validation or auditability, it should block, escalate or require human review.
