HERMETICUM B.C.E. positions IPR — Identity Primary Record as the base operational product for verifiable identity-linked workflows. The first concrete MVP is IPR AI Audit Trail: a controlled workflow for making AI-assisted document analysis traceable, human-validated and audit-ready.

1. Product hierarchy

BASE PRODUCT

IPR

IPR is the operational identity, attribution and proof record. It links subject, role, operation, event, proof, responsibility and continuity.

R&D PROFILE

IPR Base

IPR Base is a technical R&D record for internal proof flows, testing and controlled demonstrations. It does not create verified identity status.

VERIFIED PROFILE

IPR Verified

IPR Verified is the private-verification profile. It may use tax-code and valid identity-document evidence only in a controlled private layer.

PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

IPR Professional

IPR Professional is the future business / audit workflow profile based on IPR Verified, intended for controlled B2B pilots and audit-ready evidence chains.

FIRST MVP

IPR AI Audit Trail

IPR AI Audit Trail turns AI-assisted document analysis into an IPR-linked, human-validated and audit-reportable technical sequence.

ECOSYSTEM / RUNTIME / FRAMEWORK

2. Before / after

Before: weak operational identity

  • Identity is fragmented across accounts, logs and tools.
  • Audit often depends on narrative reconstruction.
  • AI-assisted outputs may lack durable traceability.
  • Responsibility is distributed across systems, teams and providers.
  • Human validation is often not connected to technical proof.

After: IPR-linked traceability

  • Operational identity is attached to the workflow.
  • Public proof references can be verified deterministically.
  • AI-assisted work becomes event-linked and proof-linked.
  • Human validation becomes part of the audit trail.
  • Missing or inconsistent evidence fails closed.

3. Capabilities

Each capability is a technical value unit: it enables safer operations, clearer responsibility and reduced audit ambiguity.

Verified operational identity

IPR Verified creates a private verification boundary for identity-linked operations while keeping public proof minimized.

Public proof minimization

The public layer stores proof references such as payload_sha256, status and timestamp, not tax codes, document numbers or identity documents.

Audit-ready AI workflows

AI-assisted document work can become traceable through IPR reference, document hash, human validation, EVT and OPC.

Fail-closed verification

Missing, malformed, inconsistent or forbidden public data must not produce validity.

Human responsibility

AI remains decision-support. Human validation and responsibility ownership stay explicit.
