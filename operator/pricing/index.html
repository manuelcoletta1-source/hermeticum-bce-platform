6. Conditions before real operator pricing

Any future commercial pricing for operator-related services should be defined only after:

  • internal MVP self-pilot reports have been reviewed;
  • at least one external pilot has been scoped;
  • legal boundaries have been reviewed;
  • security and privacy controls have been reviewed;
  • operator terminology has been clarified;
  • public proof and private evidence responsibilities have been separated;
  • no-public-data-custody requirements have been validated;
  • contractual documentation has been prepared;
  • non-claims and commercial boundaries have been approved for the target context.

7. Data and safety boundary

Any pilot discussion must use only synthetic, public, owned or expressly authorized material. Do not send secrets, private keys, credentials, identity documents, customer data, confidential third-party data, production logs, private evidence, private prompts, private outputs or sensitive operational payloads through public or preliminary channels.

Any real-data pilot requires a separate legal basis, data-processing boundary, security review, privacy review, access-control model, retention policy and written agreement.
